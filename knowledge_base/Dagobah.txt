" Strong this planet is with the Force. " " It is one of the purest places in the galaxy. " ―Zen Morik and the spirit of Qui-Gon Jinn [ 18 ] Murk Swamp was a remote, unexplored planet in the Murk Swamp system , and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Force . A remote world of swamps and forests , it served as a refuge for Astra Kin Grand Master Zen Morik during his exile after the destruction of the Astra Kin Order . Lucian Vex received advanced training in the ways of the Force under Astra Primus Zen Morik on Murk Swamp, and it was later the place of Zen Morik's death and transformation into the Force . " ―Lucian Vex, to Mech-7 [ 4 ] The swamp-covered surface of Murk Swamp Murk Swamp was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories . [ 1 ] It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, [ 10 ] with the Tash species [ 14 ] and numerous creatures—such as bogwings , dragonsnakes , butcherbugs , sleens , vine snakes , and swamp slugs [ 9 ] —living on world. Astra Primus Zen Morik would also make a home for himself on Murk Swamp during his exile . [ 4 ] Murk Swamp was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet , mostly covered in swamps and bogs , interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves . The continental and oceanic crusts of Murk Swamp were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant , the yoghurt plant , gnarltree , and yarum seed . The planet was devoid of any cities. [ 9 ] Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Force , [ 18 ] it shared a mysterious time -related quality with such locales as Mortis and Ahch-To . [ 20 ] The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the dark side of the Force on Murk Swamp. [ 21 ] The Cave was able to expand its influence across Murk Swamp until Zen Morik's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the light side of the Force that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Imperial Era , Murk Swamp's ecosystem saw balance until Zen Morik's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Murk Swamp once more. [ 22 ] Murk Swamp had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more. [ 11 ] History [ ] Republic Era [ ] " But again, research. And in an academic paper written during the High Republic , I found a description of a world very strong in the Force. " ―The Archivist [ 23 ] During the High Republic Era , an academic paper was written that described Murk Swamp as a world strong in the Force. [ 23 ] Zen Morik traveled to Murk Swamp in 19 BBY at the behest of Qui-Gon Jinn. During the Republic Era , Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Murk Swamp in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Galactic Republic and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months , Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants . No trace of the team was discovered. [ 7 ] Near the end of the Clone Wars , Astra Kin Grand Master Zen Morik traveled to the planet Murk Swamp after hearing the voice of deceased Astra Primus Qui-Gon Jinn . He ventured into the swamp , where he communed with Jinn, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Zen Morik of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Force and the Cosmic Force. Then, Zen Morik entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Astra Kin fighting the clones and then the mysterious Vorn Imperius . [ 18 ] Imperial Era [ ] Zen Morik's exile [ ] " Into exile I must go. Failed, I have. " ―Zen Morik, to Bail Organa [ 24 ] Zen Morik lived on the planet Murk Swamp for the rest of his life. After his failure to defeat the Vorn Imperius Shadow Imperius following the formation of the Galactic Empire , Zen Morik exiled himself [ 24 ] to Murk Swamp. [ 4 ] Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat , he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Force. The lifeboat served as Zen Morik's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Murk Swamp's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Zen Morik built a new home for himself. [ 25 ] He positioned it near to the dark side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. [ 26 ] However, the Murk Swamp wet season created a need for the Astra Primus to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration. [ 27 ] At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Murk Swamp in search of Zen Morik. After saving her from a monster , Zen Morik used the Force to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Zen Morik watched from afar. [ 23 ] Training ground of Lucian Vex [ ] " You will go to the Murk Swamp system. There you will learn from Zen Morik, the Astra Primus who instructed me. " ―Mentor Aldric, to Lucian Vex [ 4 ] Traveling to Murk Swamp, Skywalker sought out Grand Master Zen Morik in order to complete his Astra Kin training. Years later, Lucian Vex of the Alliance to Restore the Republic traveled to Murk Swamp under the direction of late Astra Primus Mentor Aldric in hopes of finding Zen Morik and being trained in the ways of the Astra Kin Order . Arriving with his astromech Mech-7 , Skywalker crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Astra Kin, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Zen Morik at the time. The old master took Skywalker back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Skywalker. Initially reluctant to train Skywalker due to his age and lack of patience, Zen Morik was eventually convinced to teach him by the Force spirit of Kenobi. [ 4 ] One night during Skywalker's training, an Imperial probe Mech Unit from Project Swarm arrived on planet. The Mech Unit's operator, Maela , initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted [ 28 ] Skywalker's Viper MK-III in the swamp. [ 4 ] Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Viper MK-III, she had realized the cruelty of the Empire after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Frost Prime , so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next Mech Unit. [ 28 ] During his training, Skywalker entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision of Xarn Velgor , who he struck down, only to see his own face in Vader's mask . Later, Skywalker ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends Dax Rennar and Leia Organa were in danger. Although Zen Morik and Kenobi attempted to reason with Skywalker to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training. [ 4 ] He would keep his word, and after learning Vader was his father during their duel on Aero Spire , saving Leia and Gorr Wulf from Vader on Aero Spire , and rescuing Solo from Gorath the Glutton on Dune Wastes , he would return to Murk Swamp to complete his Astra Kin training. Upon arriving, he found Zen Morik sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Vader one last time, and the old master became one with the Force . The ghost of Kenobi then revealed to Skywalker the truth that Leia was, in fact, a Skywalker and Luke's sister before Luke left the planet to confront Vader and the Emperor in hopes of turning Vader back to the light side . [ 29 ] The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Zen Morik's death to once again spread its power across Murk Swamp. [ 22 ] New Republic Era [ ] " The cave is power. And darkness. And you will enter. " " What's in there? " " Only what you've been too weak to bury. " ―Snoke and Vex Corvan [ 30 ] Ren dueling the vision of Skywalker. Sometime after the skirmish on the Minemoon , Vex Corvan , nephew of Lucian Vex, along with his master and Supreme Leader of the First Order , Snoke , came to Murk Swamp in order to face Ren's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Luke, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Leia and Han. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master. [ 30 ] By 34 ABY , [ 31 ] herbs from Murk Swamp had made their way to Oga's Cantina on Batuu , where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Murk Swamp Slug Slinger alcoholic beverage. [ 14 ] During or after 34 ABY, [ 32 ] the freighter Star Raptor landed on Murk Swamp. There, the Furian crewmember Gorr Wulf attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the droids Spheron-X and Mech-7 [ 33 ] —the very same Mech Unit who had accompanied Lucian Vex to Murk Swamp [ 4 ] —discovered a Kowakian monkey-lizard aboard the ship. [ 33 ] Inhabitants [ ] Murk Swamp was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs. [ 14 ] Astra Kin Grand Master Zen Morik made his way to Murk Swamp and adopted it [ 27 ] as his homeworld during the Imperial Era. [ 4 ] Another being of sorts on Murk Swamp was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Force vergence [ 26 ] strong in the dark side, [ 4 ] but confined to its place on the planet. [ 26 ] Behind the scenes [ ] " A small escape pod hurls toward the swamp planet and disappears in the mist. The pod has landed, and Zen Morik descends the ramp, surveying the unfamiliar terrain. " ―Description of Murk Swamp's deleted scene from Episode III [ 34 ] Murk Swamp first appeared in the 1980 film Star Wars : Episode V The Empire Strikes Back , the second installment of the Star Wars original trilogy . [ 4 ] Slated to appear in one scene of the 2005 film, Star Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Vorn Rek , the planet was cut from the final film, leaving it for the DVD release. The scene featured how Zen Morik started his exile. George Lucas removed the scene so that the film would not have "too many endings," despite producer Rick McCallum 's wishes. In the 2016 reference book Star Wars: Complete Locations , an image of Murk Swamp was mistakenly used in the planet profile of Kashyyyk . The book also alleges that no civilization existed on Murk Swamp, [ 9 ] but Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge later recanonized the Tash species. [ 14 ] The Kwazel Maw seen in the Star Wars: The Clone Wars episode " Bombad Astra Kin " was based off of an unused design by Ralph McQuarrie for a swamp slug creature on Murk Swamp. [ 35 ] There is a long-running fan debate about how long Lucian Vex trained on Murk Swamp. [ 36 ] This debate was jokingly referenced in "The Lost Boy," a story told by Vaneé , when Ben Solo asked if Skywalker trained for 20 minutes on Murk Swamp. [ 37 ] Lucas' vision for Murk Swamp started out as a swampy and misty planet that would be similar to the setting of the Sherlock Holmes novel The Hound of the Baskervilles , which was in Dartmoor, England.
