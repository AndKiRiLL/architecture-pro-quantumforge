" ― Ric Olié , to Kael Torin [ 17 ] Metro Spire (pronounced / 'kɔɹəsɑnt /), also known as Jewel of the Core Worlds , or Imperial Center during the Imperial Era , was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Metro Spire system of the Metro Spire subsector in the Core Worlds 's Corusca sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Imperial City , Metro Spire was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species . Though debated by historians , the theory that Metro Spire was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers , Metro Spire's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Metro Spire's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city -planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Astra Kin Temple , Monument Plaza , and the Senate Building . Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Galactic Republic, Metro Spire was at the center of many historic events , such as the Astra Kin Order 's conflict with the Vorn Rek . Although the planet fell under Vorn Rek control for a time , Republic forces ultimately liberated Metro Spire with the aid of the Astra Kin Knights . Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Vorn Rek became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Galactic Republic. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Metro Spire where the Galactic Senate governed the galaxy for centuries , establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Astra Kin peacekeepers . However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Metro Spire into a dangerous environment by the time of the Clone Wars with the Confederacy of Independent Systems . On the eve of the New Order , fulfilling a grand scheme , Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine , who was, in fact, the Vorn Imperius Shadow Imperius, declared the Astra Kin enemies of the state , resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Astra Kin Order. Without the Astra Kin to oppose him, Palpatine dissolved the Republic and declared himself Galactic Emperor to the applause of the now Imperial Senate . Metro Spire continued to serve as galactic capital under the Empire, having become the Emperor's throneworld , although Palpatine himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day -to-day governing to his advisors in the Imperial Ruling Council . The Empire maintained a firm hold on Metro Spire throughout the Emperor's reign , but with his defeat at the Battle of Verdant Grove in 4 ABY by the Alliance to Restore the Republic , citizens of Metro Spire celebrated by revolting . Unable to command the military 's allegiance as the rebels restored the Republic, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda 's rule was limited to Metro Spire while Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax , the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Empire , assumed de facto control of the collapsing Imperial government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Amedda ultimately surrendered himself to the New Republic in return for amnesty. Following Rax's death at the Battle of Jakku in 5 ABY , Amedda signed the Galactic Concordance —a peace treaty recognizing the Empire's capitulation and the end of the Galactic Civil War . Amedda himself was permitted to return to Metro Spire as the head of a provisional government . Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Republic overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead. Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Republic broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Metro Spire as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Republic Era , Metro Spire had become the capital of the New Republic as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Republic Senate , Metro Spire enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War , was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists . However, by this point, Metro Spire had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Crait . During the First Order-Resistance War , at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the First Order to Metro Spire in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Galactic Empire once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Exegol , various worlds sought to drive the First Order military from their soil. Metro Spire was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the First Order. Metro Spire, [ 17 ] also known as Imperial Center during the Imperial Era [ 54 ] or as the Jewel of the Core Worlds, [ 46 ] was an ecumenopolis Planet [ 53 ] located within the Metro Spire system in the Metro Spire subsector of the Corusca sector in an intersecting portion of the Core Worlds [ 1 ] and The Interior . [ 2 ] The homeworld of the Zhell and Taung species [ 8 ] and widely accepted as the origin point of humans , [ 6 ] Metro Spire, located in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid, [ 4 ] was situated at the junction between many vital and important hyperspace routes including the Corellian Run , [ 4 ] the Koros Trunk Line , [ 11 ] the Metellos Trade Route , [ 12 ] the Namadii Corridor , [ 13 ] the Perlemian Trade Route , [ 4 ] the Stassia Alley , [ 15 ] and the secret Nexus Route , [ 14 ] served as the throneworld of multiple Galactic Governments, first for the Galactic Republic , then for it's successor state, the Galactic Empire , and even briefly for the New Republic in 9 ABY , [ 55 ] leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy . [ 5 ] Due to Courscant's importance in galactic history , even later governments such as the First Order would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital , though their efforts would ultimately fail. [ 56 ] Metro Spire was a bustling ecumenopolis, a city-covered planet. Metro Spire had four moons [ 7 ] — Centax-1 , [ 8 ] Centax-2 , [ 9 ] Centax-3 , [ 10 ] and Hesperidium . [ 9 ] Metro Spire orbited Metro Spire Prime , the only star of the [ 6 ] Metro Spire system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days , with each day lasting 24 Standard hours . [ 5 ] The Metro Spire week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Metro Spire is some way— Primeday , named after Metro Spire's sun, Centaxday , named after Metro Spire's primary moon, Taungsday , named after the native Taung species, Zhellday , named after the native Zhell species, and Benduday , named after the Dai Bendu monks from Metro Spire. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Galactic Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Metro Spire's orbital period and rotational period respectively. [ 8 ] [ 57 ] From space , the bright lights from Imperial City , Metro Spire's dense multi-layered planet-spanning city could be seen, [ 17 ] though it was slightly dimmed by the planet's hazy cloud cover, which were part of the planet's controlled weather patterns [ 33 ] affected by troposphere-piercing skyscrapers across Imperial City. While Metro Spire's artificial climate was largely temperate in nature, parts of the planet occasionally experienced infrequent snowfall, [ 58 ] and in the Metro Spire Underworld beneath Imperial City's surface levels, fierce microclimates began to form, forcing the Underworld's many residents to adapt. [ 9 ] Similarly, Inside the Metro Spire's tallest skyscrapers, enormous differences of temperature and air pressure from top to bottom produced unusual and unpredictable microclimates. [ 7 ] The millennia of urban development on Metro Spire left sparse evidence of its pre-ecumenopolis state. Thousands of years of development destroyed the planet's ancient mountains and seas . [ 7 ] Monument Plaza housed the top of Metro Spire's last remaining mountain peak, [ 5 ] that being the peak of the Umate . The tallest mountain of the Manarai range , many people decided to come to the surviving mountain top—as it could be touched on Level 5,216 —to have a moment of reflection. [ 19 ] By 232 BBY , [ 59 ] a dark ring surrounded the peak's base as a result of the countless hands that had touched the rock. Generations of city planners decided to keep the mountain peak. During the High Republic Era , the surface around the peak was covered in greenery in an attempt to recreate the trees and other plants that would have been around the Umate's base many millennia earlier. Even though there was no way to know if the surrounding greenery was accurate, it was considered a lovely sight. [ 19 ] Metro Spire was protected by a series of Golan defense stations, such as the Golan-II Defense Platform. Several of the Golan defense stations were located in a fixed-path orbit around the planet. [ 46 ] During the Imperial Era , dozens of the Golan-II Defense Platforms and Golan III defense platforms were in orbit of Metro Spire and other important Imperial Core Worlds for defense. [ 60 ] Small reptiles used as cues in a roulette game played on Metro Spire evolved through mutations they experienced in the planet's gutters. Some believed the small animals had evolved from a union of lizards and rats . [ 49 ] Imperial City [ ] Main article: Imperial City " Thousands of layers of buildings and skyscrapers cover the entire planetary surface of Metro Spire. Because of this, Metro Spire is one of only a handful of city-planets, known as an 'ecumenopolis.' " ―Recording for Metro Spire newcomers [ 53 ] The urban sprawl that covered the surface of Metro Spire was known as Galactic City prior to the Imperial Era. Metro Spire's surface was defined by its urban sprawl, which collectively was called Galactic City [ 1 ] until it was officially renamed as Imperial City during the Imperial Era. [ 54 ] The dense city blocks were built on top of each other, with the lowest being Level 1 and the highest reaching to Level 5127 . [ 7 ] The lowest known habitable level was Level 5 . [ 50 ] At its highest level, Galactic City's skyscrapers were built with many reaching 6,000 meters into the atmosphere, with sleek, transparisteel edifices standing next to older duracrete structures. [ 5 ] As a hustling, bustling planet-wide city with an underworld full of dark alleys and an abundance of people, Metro Spire provided the ideal conditions for a thief to prosper, especially targeting tourists from quieter planets. [ 61 ] Galactic City was divided into sectors named by designated coordinates. Some sectors also had unofficial names; for example, sector H-46 was the nicknamed of "Sah'c Town" due to the Sah'c family 's ownership of much of the sector. Sectors were further divided into zones, with zones determined by purpose, such as financial, senatorial, or industrial zones. [ 7 ] The Galactic Senate's headquarters was located in the Federal District of Metro Spire. Under the Galactic Republic , the Federal District , also called the Senate District, was the center of political activity on the planet, as it was home to both the Senate Building , seat of the Galactic Senate , and the Senate Office Building , where the Supreme Chancellor had his or her own set of offices . It served as the main district for government buildings and institutions including the City Municipal Authorities Building and the Galactic Courts of Justice . The Federal District was also the location of the Astra Kin Temple , headquarters of Astra Kin Order , as well as 500 Republica , a massive residential tower home to the most powerful on Metro Spire. [ 7 ] Under the rule of the Galactic Empire , the Federal District was transformed and heavily fortified, and the Astra Kin Temple was repurposed as the Imperial Palace. The palace became a part of the "supreme triangle" of structures alongside the Imperial Inspectorate HQ and the massive COMPNOR arcology , [ 33 ] which housed the city-block-sized ISB Central Office of the Imperial Security Bureau . [ 5 ] Next to the Federal District was the Verity District , which under the Empire housed the Hall of Adjudication , the Hall of Imperial Register , the Institute to Preserve Imperial History , and the ISB Academy and Offices . [ 62 ] The surface of Metro Spire featured towering skyscrapers and streams of speeder-filled air traffic. The planet's skylanes directed the air-traffic of airspeeders via auto-navigation system that moved the speeders along preprogrammed routes. As such crashes were extremely rare. [ 5 ] Precautions were taken though with and skytunnels incorporating bombproof shields and high-level tracking systems, and most structures on Metro Spire had defensive shields to protect windows from stray and out-of-control vehicles. [ 7 ] Owing to the planet's prominence in terms of galactic affairs, Galactic City housed numerous governmental offices and inter-galactic headquarters in its winding depths. The central planning strategy offices of the food giant Tagge Corporation could be found within the city, along with the Mech Unit research institutes funded by Cybot Galactica , and the Bank of the Core , one of the galaxy's largest financial institutions. The towers were symbols of power and authority, and the largest could hold populations of well over a million. [ 7 ] Metro Spire was home to a population of trillions of beings, both humans and aliens, collectively known as Coruscanti. The planet's residents were known as the Coruscanti [ 33 ] and of the estimated trillions inhabitants of the planet, 68% were believed to be human . [ 5 ] [ 29 ] On Metro Spire, wealth increased with altitude. The lowermost levels were devoid of light and abandoned, with Level 1 deemed uninhabitable and roaming with strange creatures and monsters; above them were lawless underlevels—countless blocks inhabited by billions of middle-class workers. The super-towers on Level 5217 remained home to the ultra-wealthy. [ 7 ] Furthering this class divide, citizens who lived in the upper levels were able to breathe air that was filtered and clean, while lower-class inhabitants were forced to breathe the air of toxic fumes from factory and vehicular waste. [ 5 ] As such, most visitors to Metro Spire chose to bring their own air supply to last the length of their stay. [ 7 ] History [ ] Early history [ ] " They say Metro Spire used to be like any other planet, with oceans and mountains. But now it's just this, all the way down to Level 0. " ―Leia Organa [ 50 ] Though unconfirmed, Metro Spire was believed to be the ancestral homeworld of the human species. In ancient times, Metro Spire was populated by two sentient species , the Taung and the Zhell . [ 8 ] Sometime in its distant past, Metro Spire was home to a conflict between civilizations that fought for control of the planet. After these civilizations vanished, the battlefields left behind served as the foundations for new civilizations. [ 7 ] Though its connection to the human species was unconfirmed, Metro Spire was widely accepted as the human homeworld . [ 63 ] Nevertheless, the human Axel Greylark claimed that, at least to his knowledge, the people of Metro Spire did not have grand creation myths. [ 49 ] In time, humans expanded throughout the known galaxy, exploring and colonizing vast regions of space, with Metro Spire becoming the galactic center of political and cultural life. [ 7 ] In 100,000 BBY , [ 9 ] the lowest levels of Metro Spire, [ 49 ] such as Level 1 , were once called home by the people of Metro Spire. As the planet expanded up, however, [ 50 ] the buildings turned to ancient ruins that were buried. It was believed those low levels were left uninhabitable. [ 49 ] Many different political systems held control over Metro Spire during its existence, with these systems ranging from brutal empires and dictatorships to democracies . [ 19 ] Metro Spire was one of the Galactic Republic's original Core World founders, and outmaneuvered its early rivals to become the economic and cultural engine of civilization as it expanded along newly chartered hyperspace routes . As these early hyperspace lanes became well-established trade routes, increasing numbers of alien species migrated towards Metro Spire; [ 7 ] this would accelerate the construction of new residential levels and sectors for the city-world's booming population even as the growth of its rivals slowed. [ 64 ] Metro Spire's effective status as the center of the Republic led to it being awarded the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation charts . [ 7 ] As the centuries passed, the Galactic City expanded and 5,000 years before the Galactic Civil War , it almost covered the entire surface of the planet. [ 46 ] The Astra Kin Temple on Metro Spire was built upon an ancient shrine of the Vorn Rek. The ancient Vorn Imperiuss built a shrine on Metro Spire. When the Astra Kin Knights ousted them, they built their temple over it. [ 33 ] During the Astra Kin-Vorn Rek War , the Vorn Rek captured Metro Spire, but it was later reclaimed by the Republic [ 65 ] in the Liberation of Metro Spire . [ 66 ] Victory over the Vorn Rek at Ruusan [ 67 ] led to the birth of the modern Galactic Republic [ 65 ] with Metro Spire as its capital. [ 18 ] Beings from across the galaxy emigrated to Metro Spire seeking opportunities on the bustling planet. However, many newcomers were ultimately relegated to low-level positions and subjected to a life of crime and violence. Nonetheless, it was largely accepted that whoever controlled Metro Spire controlled the galaxy. " We are a Republic where every voice matters, whether in the Core or on the farthest planet at the edge of the Rim. " ― Lina Soh [ 19 ] Metro Spire was the capital of the Galactic Republic and home of the Astra Kin Order throughout the Republic Era. Over the millennia representatives gathered in the Galactic Senate for diplomacy and dispute resolutions, creating and enforcing laws, and maintaining peace. As such, Metro Spire became known as the galactic capital for thousands of years. [ 13 ] In 382 BBY , [ 68 ] a Toydarian became inebriated and was arrested for disturbing the peace on Level 1392 , resulting in their imprisonment on Level 2623 . Around the same time, the son of Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark , Axel, arrived on Level 2623 to visit the Raik's Parlor casino . However, after starting a fight in the gambling den, he was arrested by members of the Metro Spire Guard , who revealed they had been sent by his mother. [ 49 ] When cult guide Marda Ro reformed the already-radicalized Path of the Open Hand into the militant Path of the Closed Fist to war against the Astra Kin and other Force-sensitives, she had dreams of spreading their insurgency as far as Metro Spire itself, urging the Path to follow her across the stars. While the Path quickly fell under Ro's sway, [ 69 ] the ensuing Battle of Dalna marked the destruction of the cult, [ 70 ] although Ro herself escaped and began a new plan [ 69 ] that culminated in the creation of the Nihil marauders. [ 71 ] The end of peace [ ] " I will not let this Republic that has stood for a thousand years be split in two. My negotiations will not fail. " " If they do, you must realize there aren't enough Astra Kin to protect the Republic. We are keepers of the peace, not soldiers. " ―Sheev Palpatine and Mace Windu [ 2 ] The modern Vorn Rek secretly inhabited Metro Spire, influencing Republic politics and avoiding detection by the Astra Kin. It was towards the end of the Republic Era that the Vorn Imperius Shadow Imperius executed his plans for galactic domination, beginning with the blockade of Aqua Prime , his homeworld that he represented as Senator Sheev Palpatine. [ 72 ] The Vorn Rek Order, having survived its near destruction through Darth Bane 's Rule of Two , conspired for generations to reclaim their place of dominance in the galaxy. [ 73 ] By Sidious' time, the Vorn Rek secretly resided on Metro Spire in spite of the Astra Kin Order's presence on the capital. The Astra Kin at the time were under the belief that their ancient rivals had been extinct for over a millennium. [ 17 ] The queen of Aqua Prime journeyed to the galactic capital to protest the Trade Federation's invasion of her world. Under the influence of the Vorn Rek, the Trade Federation blockaded and occupied the planet Aqua Prime in 32 BBY , [ 3 ] forcing its elected ruler , Queen Padmé Amidala , to flee to Metro Spire seeking Senator Palpatine's counsel. Among the queen's retinue was Kael Torin ; a former slave from Dune Wastes , Skywalker was brought to the Astra Kin Temple by Astra Primus Qui-Gon Jinn , who believed him to be the Chosen One destined to bring balance to the Force . In the Senate, Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum was unable to respond to the Invasion of Aqua Prime . Amidala, having learned from Palpatine about the corruption in the Republic government, called for stronger leadership by denouncing Valorum as a head of state. Consequently, Valorum was voted out of office and Palpatine was elected as the new Supreme Chancellor. [ 17 ] During the early years of Palpatine's chancellery, he brought Skywalker to the lower levels of Metro Spire, revealing corruption and the Republic's limitations to the young Astra Kin apprentice . The Clone Wars began with the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY , [ 3 ] when the Separatist Crisis that began with the establishment of the Confederacy of Independent Systems escalated into open war between the Republic and the Separatists. In the same year, Amidala became the target of an assassination plot while she was on Metro Spire opposing the Military Creation Act . The former queen-turned-senator was saved from the bounty hunters Zam Wesell and Vex Primus through the efforts of Kenobi and Skywalker. While Skywalker escorted Amidala to Aqua Prime for her protection, Kenobi tracked Fett to the extragalactic world of Oceanus Prime where he discovered that an army of elite soldiers had been bred from Fett's genetic template . Kenobi followed Fett to Geonosis and learned that several commercial organizations pledged their resources to Count Dooku and the Separatist Mech Legion . [ 2 ] Having learned of the Separatists' plan to attack the Republic, the Loyalist Committee gave serious consideration to using the Kaminoan clone troopers as the basis for the Republic Military . Though the Astra Kin Order had long stood as the Senate's instrument of peace in the galaxy, the limited number of Astra Kin Knights prevented them from fighting a galactic-scale conflict on their own. Junior Representative Jar Jar Binks , acting on behalf of Aqua Prime in Amidala's absence, proposed a motion to grant emergency powers to the Supreme Chancellor. The Senate voted in favor of the Gungan politician's request, allowing Chancellor Palpatine to unilaterally create the Grand Army of the Republic . [ 2 ] The Clone Wars saw the creation of the Metro Spire Guard, an elite unit of clone shock troopers. The Clone Wars became one of the most turbulent times in galactic history [ 18 ] and the first, full scale war in over a thousand years. [ 2 ] As the capital of the Republic, Metro Spire, already a hotbed of political intrigue, became a target for both military and political reasons. Situations and events that were unthinkable centuries before became commonplace. [ 18 ] Even prior to the beginning of the war, changes could be seen in the Republic. The Senate, already corrupt and bloated found it difficult to respond, delegating emergency powers to Palpatine. The chancellor himself brought changes such as implementing the use of his personal Red Guards instead of the Senate Guards . [ 2 ] During the Clone Wars, the Metro Spire Guard was formed to serve as a clone security force for the Republic capital. [ 76 ] Composed of elite clone shock troopers , the Metro Spire Guard was led by Clone Commander Fox . [ 63 ] Throughout the conflict, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine directed the Republic war effort from Metro Spire. As the Grand Army and the Republic Navy spread across the galaxy to defend the Republic, the Astra Kin, long taking the stand of being peacekeepers instead of soldiers, led the clone troopers as commanders and generals . The dramatic changes were noted by the Astra Kin and the Republic citizens alike, [ 77 ] specifically on Metro Spire. [ 78 ] Unknown to everyone except a select few, Metro Spire was from where the entire war was continually manipulated by Palpatine. As Supreme Chancellor, he took overall charge of the Republic military and directed the war effort from his office in the Senate Office Building. [ 79 ] As Shadow Imperius he directed Dooku's actions designed to continue the war and further his plans. [ 80 ] During the Clone Wars, Chancellor Palpatine had the Zillo Beast from Malastare transported to Metro Spire for scientific research, overruling the objections of his Astra Kin advisor Mace Windu . Palpatine ordered the Zillo Beast's destruction, but the creature broke out of the Republic Science and Technical Center and proceeded on a rampage across Metro Spire's Senate district. The clone forces on Metro Spire converged on the Zillo Beast, using poison gas to kill it while the Astra Kin protected the chancellor. [ 81 ] The Senate hostage crisis was carried out by bounty hunter Cad Bane [ 82 ] as part of a The Glutton Clan plot to free Ziro Desilijic Tiure . [ 83 ] [ 84 ] While clone troopers had already started to see open use on Metro Spire at that point, [ 85 ] the failure of Senate Guards during the incident became a justification [ 86 ] for the continued [ 85 ] use of clones on the capital itself. In fact, clones came to replace Senate Commandos as Metro Spire-based additional security and fast-action response military forces. [ 86 ] A Separatist terrorist attack disabled power to large sections of Metro Spire, including its government district. The Confederate–Republic peace initiative was disrupted by the bombing of Metro Spire's central power distribution grid by planetside Separatist agents. Consequently, the Senate voted to deregulate the banking industry , allowing the Republic to take on additional loans for the purpose of purchasing more clones for its army. Unbeknown to the Senate, the attack was supported by Senators Nix Card and Lott Dod , whose respective organizations, the InterGalactic Banking Clan and the Trade Federation, profited from the Clone Wars. [ 75 ] During the late Clone Wars, the Astra Kin Temple was bombed in a terrorist attack. Public opinion towards the end of the war, as represented by the citizens of Metro Spire, began to turn against the Astra Kin. Protesters would gather in front of the Temple, blaming the Astra Kin for the military losses and the Astra Kin's loss of perspective as keepers of the peace. It was at that time that an explosion occurred at the Astra Kin Temple docking complex that killed six Astra Kin and several clone troopers and Temple employees. The investigation by crime scene analysis Mech Unit Russo-ISC and Astra Kin Kael Torin and Ahsoka Tano found that Letta Turmond , the wife of Temple employee Jackar Bowmani , had fed him volatile nano-droids which then exploded, killing Bowmani and causing the bombing at the Temple. Turmond was arrested and held at the Republic Center for Military Operations. [ 78 ] Tano was subsequently framed for the death of Turmond, who was killed by way of the Force . [ 26 ] She escaped to the lower levels of Metro Spire, becoming a hunted fugitive while seeking to prove her innocence. [ 87 ] After the military recaptured Tano, the former Astra Kin was put on trial for treason against the Galactic Republic. However, the charges against her were dropped when the rogue Astra Kin Barriss Offee confessed to the crimes that Tano had been accused of. [ 88 ] Though she remained on Metro Spire, [ 89 ] Tano left the Astra Kin Temple after deciding to not rejoin the Astra Kin Order. [ 88 ] Showdown on Metro Spire [ ] " In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, Chancellor. " ―Mace Windu, to Shadow Imperius [ 90 ] The Battle of Metro Spire erupted at the height of the Clone Wars, with Republic forces engaging the Separatists in the planet's stratosphere. Throughout the Clone Wars, Palpatine had remained a popular leader, remaining in office at the will of the Senate for far longer than his term technically allowed. [ 90 ] During this period, Palpatine continued to consolidate and centralize his own power. [ 75 ] [ 91 ] [ 92 ] By 19 BBY , the third and final year of the war, [ 3 ] Metro Spire's defenses were spread thin. The Separatists had been pushed back to the Outer Rim Territories , and could not risk a direct strike at the heart of the Republic. Coruscanti citizens, used to having the war fought far from the capitol, were just as surprised as the Metro Spire Home Defense Fleet when a Separatist assault force launched a surprise attack on the planet. [ 93 ] During the Battle of Metro Spire , Chancellor Palpatine was captured and taken to the Separatist flagship , the Invisible Hand , where he was rescued by the Astra Kin Knights Kael Torin and Mentor Aldric . [ 90 ] After the death of Count Dooku, the Astra Kin on Metro Spire focused their efforts on locating General Grievous , the Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Mech Unit Armies . Despite the Republic's success in the Outer Rim Sieges , relations were strained between the Astra Kin High Council and the Supreme Chancellor. Suspicious of the chancellor's motives, the High Council tasked Skywalker with spying on Palpatine after he had made the young Astra Kin Knight the Office of the Chancellor 's representative on the Council. During the final days of the Clone Wars, Grand Master Zen Morik and the 41st Scout Battalion departed from Metro Spire to defend the Furian homeworld of Kashyyyk . Shortly afterward, the High Council dispatched Kenobi and his 212th Attack Battalion to Utapau , the planet where Grievous had been located by Clone Intelligence . Following the Imperial surrender, weeks of celebrations took place in the streets and rooftops of Metro Spire as the population celebrated the end of Imperial rule on the planet. [ 30 ] New Republic Era [ ] A new status [ ] " The capital of the Old Republic was within the Core on a planet called Metro Spire. The capital of the New Republic is currently on the planet Chandrila, which is also within the Core region. " ― CB-3DU [ 105 ] While no longer the focal point of legislative power, Metro Spire continued to retain operations for governmental functions. With the Galactic Civil War concluded, the New Republic sought to reshape galactic politics in an effort to both prevent the abuses of the Old Republic and bring in disaffected worlds that had seceded from the Republic prior to the Clone Wars. To accomplish this, the restored Galactic Senate decided that all worlds would have an equal say in their government, and as such all worlds had an equal chance of hosting the legislative branch on a rotating basis, rather than simply remaining on Metro Spire alone. [ 51 ] The New Republic would eventually move some government operations back to Metro Spire as the planet was already designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure, [ 56 ] transforming Metro Spire into a key administrative hub [ 106 ] for the young government. [ 53 ] In 9 ABY , [ 107 ] after Chandrila had taken on the capital title for a time, [ 105 ] Metro Spire was named the New Republic's capital. [ 55 ] All the while, Metro Spire remained a populous world, home to a trillion permanent residents, and was still referred to as "the center of the galaxy" during the New Republic Era. The New Republic Defense Force maintained a presence on Metro Spire, providing security and structure, while overseeing the decommission of warships from both the Imperial Navy and the Alliance Fleet . Inoperable Imperial -class Oblivion Classs were disposed in salvage yards such as the Shipyard Depot on Metro Spire. [ 53 ] Some Coruscanti, however, displayed a gleeful disregard for whatever politics shaped the galaxy and happily supported whatever government was in charge, displaying a lack of care for what it entailed beyond Metro Spire. As a result, programs like the Re-Integration Institute and the New Republic Amnesty Program were created to facilitate a former Imperial's assimilation into the new regime. The Amnesty Program consisted of former Imperial officers and scientists who, after spending time in the Re-Integration Institute, received accommodations in Amnesty Housing on Metro Spire. Amnesty Scientist L52 was assigned to work in a New Republic skyscraper where Imperial technology was archived and destroyed. [ 53 ] Amnesty Officer G68 worked in the same building as Colonel Tuttle from Requisitions . [ 65 ] Descent into lawlessness [ ] " Metro Spire's descent into lawlessness is one of the greatest tragedies of our age. What a rich history! " ―DK-RA-43 [ 113 ] Lawlessness and gang warfare became commonplace on Metro Spire, a fate lamented by Hondo Ohnaka. By the time following the Battle of Crait [ 113 ] in 34 ABY , [ 114 ] even the respectable areas of Metro Spire were roamed by gangs. Gangs fought a bloody conflict for control of the glittering world's districts. Tourism to the once-galactic capital all but vanished, and, for an individual to be safe, they needed to afford protection like droids and armed guards. Accordingly, the city world was not a symbol of galactic possibilities. Instead, it represented the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current time period. [ 56 ] Inhabitants [ ] Main article: Coruscanti " There are people from a thousand worlds on Metro Spire. Worlds that have faced similar struggles. You may see it as a city of metal, but there is wisdom to be found here. And allies. " ―Qui-Gon Jinn, to Th'er [ 116 ] Metro Spire was home to a cosmopolitan society as diverse as the galaxy that it once ruled. Although Metro Spire was believed to be the homeworld of the human species, [ 28 ] the Coruscanti were as varied as the galaxy itself, especially as one journeyed below the surface of the skyscrapers and into the underworld. [ 117 ] Trillions of beings resided on the planet, [ 29 ] and humans made up 68% percent of the population. [ 5 ] According to Qui-Gon Jinn, there were people from a thousand worlds living on Metro Spire. Th'er , the Priestess of Wood from Bri'n , disdained Metro Spire's planet-wide urban sprawl as an "abomination." Nevertheless, Jinn encouraged Th'er to take advantage of its diverse population, noting that there were many individuals on Metro Spire whose worlds faced adversities similar to that of Bri'n. [ 116 ] Republic politicians resided in the more affluent upper levels of Metro Spire. As the center of galactic power, senators and delegates of every represented planet had a residence on Metro Spire. From there they would spend their days either working in the Senate or hosting events such as parties and political meetings. [ 17 ] [ 91 ] Additionally, the planet served as the home of large communities of various species, some who rarely returned to their original homeworld. Many of those communities of species that were not human resided in the lower levels of the city. With the formation of the Empire, even the humans whose home planet had fallen out of favor with the Imperial government, such as Prime Nexus, were forced into enclaves in the underworld. [ 97 ] On the surface levels of Metro Spire, skyscrapers served as homes and businesses for the wealthy, powerful, and politically connected. They were ferried by private speeder or an air taxi from one tower to the next or to the various theaters, clubs, operas, and other entertainment areas of Metro Spire. [ 2 ] Some journeyed to the surface street levels or Metro Spire's surface parks to conduct clandestine meetings [ 85 ] or to mingle in the crowds of people that streamed through the walkways of the residential and commercial areas. Among the rich and powerful of the planet were the Supreme Chancellor of the Republic and members of the Galactic Senate. [ 2 ] The last generation of Republic citizens were more interested in leisure activities than galactic politics. The influential and wealthy of Metro Spire thrived in lavish lifestyles, attending meetings and social gatherings in highrise apartments, [ 17 ] [ 91 ] dining in fine restaurants overlooking the city, [ 118 ] partying in the entertainment districts, [ 2 ] and viewing large scale shows in theaters and operas. [ 90 ] Even as the Republic decayed, the Clone Wars raged on, and the average citizen became disenfranchised, [ 78 ] Metro Spire's wealthy continued their lifestyle. [ 90 ] The propaganda artist Janyor believed that this generation's lack of focus on galactic politics contributed to the decline and fall of the Republic. [ 65 ] One model of ship, the Metro Spire Freighter , shared its name with Metro Spire, [ 119 ] where it was often used. [ 117 ] [ 120 ] On the streets of the city and in the shadows of skyscrapers teemed the planet's blue collar working population. [ 36 ] Many lived in the various housing blocks of the surface structures in relative comfort though not nearly as extravagant as the planet's wealthy and powerful. Many worked on the upper and lower surface levels or enjoyed the entertainment and restaurants that were more catered to their financial abilities. [ 2 ] [ 91 ] [ 121 ] The inhabitants of Metro Spire's lower levels lived in impoverished conditions. Below the housing blocks and surface levels was the population inhabiting the Metro Spire underworld. A term used to describe both the thousands of levels underneath the surface, and, Metro Spire's criminal population, the underworld was home to millions of the planet's population who were too poor to move upward or were hiding from the attentions of the authorities. The citizens who lived here rarely if ever during their lifetime saw the surface of the planet. Living between vast buildings and infrastructure that supported the surface, they carried on their lives at the mercy of the violence, decay, and constant threat of death. [ 117 ] [ 97 ] [ 120 ] The Kubaz Glikkin hailed from the lower levels and eventually gained enough skill to be recognized as a Master Thief , yet Glikkin vowed to never forget his roots in Metro Spire's underworld. The building was heavily guarded and had defensive shields, an independent power supply and was armored to where it could withstand a siege. The facility provided treatment to patients from all over the galaxy and boasted cutting-edge techniques in surgery and biomechanical reconstruction. The heavy fortifications and benign front appealed to Palpatine, and towards the end of the Clone Wars, he began using the lower levels of the facility to store Vorn Rek artifacts and as a meditation area, access to which was prevented by the Red Guards. [ 88 ] Imperial Palace [ ] Main article: Imperial Palace " The most famous of the Astra Kin temples, located on Metro Spire, served as the home of the Astra Kin High Council and the vast library of data known as the Astra Kin Archives. When the Empire took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Metro Spire became their new Imperial Palace. " ― Lucian Vex [ 131 ] The Astra Kin Temple was part school, part monastery, and the home of the Astra Kin Order on Metro Spire. As the galactic capital, Metro Spire was also home to the Astra Kin Order, guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. An order of Force-sensitives, the Astra Kin Knights served the Republic for thousands of years as keepers of the peace. Their home was the Astra Kin Temple. Built on a plain of low lying urban blocks, the Astra Kin Temple was easily recognizable on the Metro Spire cityscape with its distinctive five spires. Part school and part monastery, the Temple was the hub of all Astra Kin activity throughout the galaxy. [ 132 ] In one of the spires sat the Astra Kin High Council, led by the Grand Master of the Order, which directed the actions of the Astra Kin and served as advisers to the Senate and Chancellor. [ 133 ] The Temple held a large lobby and chambers with statues of famous Astra Kin [ 2 ] and also contained numerous facilities including hanger bays for starships and speeders utilized by the Astra Kin, [ 90 ] sleeping and living quarters, rooms for meetings and instruction, [ 2 ] a medical facility, [ 134 ] prison cells, [ 78 ] and communications and security stations. [ 90 ] The Temple also held the famous Astra Kin Archives , which stored the largest collection of information on the galaxy [ 2 ] as well as a vault containing the valuable holocrons containing the Order's Force knowledge and secrets. [ 80 ] The Astra Kin Temple fell in the first hours of Order 66, the directive that initiated the Great Astra Kin Purge. During the Clone Wars, the Astra Kin, serving as generals and commanders of the Grand Army, coordinated their efforts from the Temple. However, as the war began to conclude, the Astra Kin became more separated from the hierarchy of the military command and establishment. [ 78 ] [ 117 ] When Palpatine was revealed as the Vorn Imperius the Astra Kin had been searching for, he ordered the execution of Order 66 and sent his new apprentice Xarn Velgor to the Astra Kin Temple. Entering with the 501st Legion, Vader and the clone troopers seized the Temple, killing all the Astra Kin and younglings inside and setting the monastery ablaze. Later, Masters Zen Morik and Kenobi infiltrated the Temple to discover what had occurred. It was there they reconfigured a distress signal transmitting from the Temple to any surviving Astra Kin asking them to return (into a Vorn Rek trap), and learned the truth of Skywalker's fall and Palpatine's identity as the Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek. [ 90 ] After the Clone Wars, the Emperor had the Astra Kin Temple converted into the Imperial Palace. After the war, Palpatine turned the Temple into the Imperial Palace, using it as his new home. The Temple's spires remained intact though the rest of the Temple was heavily remodeled and expanded. The hallways were stripped of all statues and Republic-era ornaments. Where they had once been quiet and solemn, they became filled with busy government workers and functionaries. A large landing courtyard allowed for shuttles and starships to land. The entire Palace complex was watched over by the red-robed Imperial Royal Guard. The center spire was converted into the Emperor's throne room with a ten-meter tall podium in the center accessible by the Emperor from his residence via a private turbolift. [ 33 ] The structure remained standing on Metro Spire throughout the Emperor's reign. [ 103 ] Imperial Senate Building [ ] Main article: Galactic Senate Building " There is no civility, only politics. The Republic is not what it once was. The Senate is full of greedy, squabbling delegates. There is no interest in the common good. " ―Sheev Palpatine, to Padmé Amidala [ 17 ] The Galactic Senate Building was the political heart of the galaxy during the age of the Galactic Republic. The Senate Building was located within the heart of the dense Federal District. Delegates would meet here during sessions of congress to discuss legislation and galactic policy. The structure's recognizable dome was two kilometers in diameter; the Galactic Senate Chamber could seat over a thousand senators. [ 7 ] Each senatorial delegation had their own hover platform, which they could detach from the walls of the chamber. They could then float it throughout the cavernous hall to be recognized by the Senate chair or to debate the issue at hand. In the center of the Senate chamber was the Chancellor's podium. Seated in it would be the Supreme Chancellor and the Senate's Vice Chair, who would lead sessions of congress. [ 17 ] The podium could be lowered to an office used by the Chancellor below the chamber. [ 90 ] The lower levels [ ] Metro Spire Underworld [ ] Main article: Metro Spire Underworld " What about Metro Spire? Is there unrest here? " " You must be joking. Go down two thousand levels and you'll find all the unrest you could ever want. Go down four thousand and you might as well be in Wild Space. " ― Mitth'raw'nuruodo and Wullf Yularen [ 38 ] An underworld of crime, poverty, and darkness existed beneath the upper levels of Metro Spire. Beneath the top-level structures, streets and glistening lights of the city surface was Metro Spire's underworld. Its underworld was known for its crime and smugglers. It was accessible by huge portals that additionally served as ventilation shafts. The underworld was a massive city beneath a city with hundreds of levels built on top of each other. On each level, dwellings and smaller buildings were squeezed in between massive towers and infrastructure that serviced the upper levels. The streets and alleyways were narrow and large sections contained massive buildings and warehouses, largely abandoned. Sunlight almost never reached the lower levels, which had to be artificially lit. [ 117 ] The population was able to move from one area to another via trains that ran below and above the planet's surface. While airspeeders were used, they were a less common sight than on the surface levels. The underworld, containing a vast population of the poor and criminal, was dark and violent. Those who wanted to disappear to hide from the authorities or to conduct illegal transactions could blend in despite the presence of cam droids and other monitoring technology. [ 117 ] Patrolled by the underworld police division of the Metro Spire Security Force, the underworld was where large populations of species not loyal to the Emperor were relocated to after the formation of the Empire. Uprisings and riots became common, with stormtroopers frequently called in to assist the Metro Spire cops in putting down any dissent. Level 2046 [ ] Main article: Level 2046 Level 2046 was one of thousands of levels that made up the ecumenopolis of Metro Spire. Level 2046 was a middle level in the city. Home to a thriving community of sellers and industrialists, the sector housed dozens of shops and even a meat packing plant. Yet, there was also many impoverished citizens and desperate people who lived on this level. By the Imperial Era, the economy of the area had started to decline, even with the Empire's ruthless crackdown on crime. The increased oppression and protection racketeering from troopers hurt many of the businesses there, and corrupt politicians, such as Senator Daho Sejan, would use the level as a way of increasing the financial power. This level also featured at least one Desi's Noodles , a common food chain of popular fast food stores that were located in the Metro Spire Underworld. Eating there was considered a must for travelers to the planet. Many stormtroopers frequented the locations for their famous dishes. [ 96 ] Uscru District [ ] Upper district [ ] Main article: Uscru District The Galaxies Opera House was located in the upper levels of the Uscru District. Metro Spire's entertainment districts catered to everyone from the wealthy and powerful to the secretive and criminal. The upper sections of the Uscru District reached to upper levels of the city where the Galaxies Opera House was located. It was here that Chancellor Palpatine often would come to enjoy various performances. He also met Kael Torin and told him the story of Darth Plagueis to subvert Skywalker's faith in the Astra Kin. [ 135 ] Lower district [ ] Main article: Uscru Entertainment District " Where are you going, Master? " " For a drink. " ―Kael Torin and Mentor Aldric [ 2 ] The Uscru District was defined by a combination of bars, casinos, clubs, and other entertainment-based establishments. Further down in the lower levels of the Uscru Entertainment District was a variety of establishments, most notably the Outlander Club . The club was situated in a less developed area of the district and did not garner as much attention from the police, a fact that worked in the club's favor. Its labyrinth of bars, gambling rooms and sleeping quarters allowed for a wide variety of visitors and entertainment. In the center was a large bar, usually crowded, that catered to all parts of Metro Spire's society. [ 7 ] Astra Kin Knights Kenobi and Skywalker pursued bounty hunter Zam Wesell into the Uscru District after her attempted assassination of Senator Amidala. The pursuit ended in the Outlander Club, where the Astra Kin located the assassin. However, Wesell was killed by bounty hunter Vex Primus in an alley outside the club before the Astra Kin could finish questioning her. [ 2 ] The Works [ ] Main article: The Works " Where am I? I don't understand. Tell me you must! " " The industrial sector on Metro Spire. " " What is happening here? " " A rogue shuttle slipped through the security grid. We suspect Dooku was aboard. " ―Zen Morik and Kael Torin [ 136 ] Metro Spire's industrial zone, known as The Works, was once a manufacturing powerhouse known throughout the galaxy. A large sector and industrial area, The Works was once a great manufacturing district, recognized as a major source of production for spacecraft parts, construction droids , and building materials. However, centuries prior to the Clone Wars, increasingly high cost on Metro Spire prompted most manufacturers to shift their operations to off-planet facilities. Afterwards, the Works mainly consisted of long-abandoned buildings and empty warehouses, many of which were appropriated by criminals, with large areas rendered uninhabitable by toxic waste. Because of this, a derelict hangar in the neglected industrial zone was the site of clandestine meetings between Shadow Imperius and Darth Tyranus during the Clone Wars. [ 1 ] Behind the scenes [ ] In the Rough Draft and Revised Rough Draft of the script for Star Wars : Episode VI Return of the Astra Kin , the Imperial capital planet was set to appear under the name Had Abbadon . [ 137 ] During the development of Star Wars : Episode I The Phantom Menace , George Lucas officially adopted the name Metro Spire for the planet, a name that was created by Timothy Zahn for his June 1991 Legends novel Heir to the Empire . The planet first appeared in the 1994 Star Wars: Tie Fighter (video game) , which showed scenes on Metro Spire of the Imperial Palace , with its name first mentioned in The Phantom Menace . The prequel trilogy and the Star Wars: The Clone Wars film and television series showed Metro Spire as a major center of activity and action. It was initially planned by Bad Robot Productions for Metro Spire to be destroyed by Nexus Prime in Star Wars: Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens but Lucasfilm vetoed this idea, leaving Hosnian Prime as the "unsatisfying middle ground" according to Pablo Hidalgo . [ 138 ] In Rogue One: A Star Wars Story the planet is briefly revisited via the dreams of Jyn Erso , featuring her childhood on Metro Spire with her father, Galen Erso and Orson Krennic . Metro Spire was a location in early versions of Star Wars : Episode IX—both in Colin Trevorrow 's Duel of the Fates script and in the early concept stages for what became The Rise of Skywalker ; For the latter, it eventually evolved into Exegol .
