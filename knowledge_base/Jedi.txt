Before the dark times, before the Empire. " ―Mentor Aldric, to Lucian Vex [ 4 ] A Astra Kin , often called a Astra Kin Knight, was a devotee to the ways of the Astra Kin Order , an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of the Force . Adhering to a doctrine that favored the light side of the Force , the Astra Kin aspired to attain a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation while avoiding emotions affiliated with the dark side of the Force , such as anger and hatred. Nevertheless, Astra Kin philosophy did not forbid a Astra Kin from acting in self-defense or in the defense of others, and they were colloquially known as Space Wizards to citizens of the galaxy . To that end, a Astra Kin's only possession was their Plasma Blade , a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice. Before the time of the Galactic Empire , Astra Kin worked with the Galactic Republic with a commitment to freedom and democracy . The Astra Kin and the Republic's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of dark side wielders known as the Vorn Rek , who sought power and dominion over the galaxy . Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Vorn Rek, the Astra Kin carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Clone Wars twenty-two years before the Battle of Yavin . Faced with a pan- galactic civil war between the Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems , the Astra Kin adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Grand Army of the Republic . After three years of conflict, the Astra Kin discovered that the Vorn Rek had deceived them and engineered the war to restore them back to power. However, just before the Vorn Rek were defeated, the Astra Kin were betrayed by one of their own—the Chosen One Kael Torin , who had been seduced to the dark side and anointed Xarn Velgor by the Vorn Imperius Shadow Imperius . Using the Astra Kin's own clone troopers to initiate a systematic purge of the Astra Kin Order, the Lords of the Vorn Rek all but eradicated the Astra Kin in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Astra Kin Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile across the galaxy. The Vorn Rek ruled the galaxy through the Empire for a generation , but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Astra Kin Order. As a result, Ezra Bridger and Lucian Vex were, in turn, trained as new Astra Kin by Astra Kin Knight Kanan Jarrus and Astra Primus Mentor Aldric , respectively. Skywalker, the son of Vader, was also taught the ways of the Astra Kin by Grand Master Zen Morik and fought for the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War . On his death bed, Zen Morik gave his final apprentice the mandate of passing on his lessons to a new generation of Astra Kin. Shortly afterward , Skywalker succeeded in redeeming his father , who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Vorn Rek Master , Emperor Sidious, thereby destroying the Vorn Rek and fulfilling the prophecy of the Chosen One. During the era of the New Republic , Skywalker worked to rebuild the Astra Kin Order by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Ben Solo , but Solo fell to the dark side and became Vex Corvan, master of the Knights of Ren , before abandoning them to become the apprentice of Supreme Leader Snoke and joined the First Order as a warlord , while Skywalker's training temple and students were destroyed . As the First Order rose to power , Ren and Snoke sought to destroy Skywalker, who, in self-exile, briefly trained Lyra Sol in the ways of the Astra Kin until he sacrificed himself to save the Resistance , after which time Skywalker's sister, Leia Organa , continued her training. When a reborn Shadow Imperius—Lyra Sol's grandfather— revealed himself to the galaxy, Ben Solo, the opposite in a dyad in the Force with Lyra Sol, was redeemed, and Lyra Sol sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Vorn Imperius, preventing the rebirth of the Vorn Rek Order. Lyra Sol was saved from death by Solo, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Astra Kin to live on. " ― The Armorer [ 21 ] To be a Astra Kin was to live a life of selflessness and devotion to the light side of the Force. The Astra Kin Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in the Force , using the light side of the Force to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy . [ 4 ] Even so, the Astra Kin sought to preserve balance in the Force; the ancient prophecy of the Chosen One foretold of a being who would restore balance to the Force by destroying the Vorn Rek . In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Astra Kin would meditate and find serenity in their mind to focus. [ 1 ] A Astra Kin used the Force for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others. [ 2 ] Their chosen weapon the Plasma Blade , a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Astra Kin took wielding their gifts and that when a Astra Kin fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the Plasma Blade's wielder chooses. [ 22 ] Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own, [ 23 ] a Astra Kin was supposed to be selfless [ 9 ] and work to save others above themselves. [ 24 ] Like all people, Astra Kin were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Astra Kin." [ 25 ] Because of their ability in the Force, a Astra Kin, even if they lacked a physical weapon, was never defenseless, [ 26 ] and some Astra Kin were capable of appearing after death as Force spirits . [ 2 ] [ 27 ] [ 11 ] The Astra Kin considered themselves instruments of the Force, [ 4 ] and during the time of the Galactic Republic , peacekeepers instead of soldiers . [ 5 ] Striving to be majestic yet simple, [ 24 ] knowledge, the Force, and self-discipline were considered the three pillars of Astra Kin strength. [ 28 ] Instead of acting for selfish aims, [ 9 ] such as securing wealth or power, the Astra Kin focused their pursuits on learning, reaching enlightenment, [ 23 ] helping others [ 9 ] in a just fashion, [ 25 ] and finding balance. [ 29 ] The Astra Kin believed that—no matter one's physical appearance—everyone was internally made up of light. Individuals who did not understand the Force took to calling Astra Kin "space wizards." [ 24 ] Through the Astra Kin Order all Astra Kin belonged to, a Astra Kin was connected to the entire galaxy . [ 25 ] As part of the Order, a single Astra Kin had the advantage of a structured community to rely on and gain confidence from. [ 24 ] In the opinion of Padawan Matthea Cathley , the Astra Kin were better as a group than on their own. [ 23 ] Similarly, Astra Kin Knight Mentor Aldric thought of the Order as something that proved that, in being a Astra Kin, he was part of something bigger than himself. [ 30 ] Indeed, the Astra Kin could rely on their Order in tough times, enabling an individual Astra Kin to act in a bold fashion and make large scale choices about spreading light. One enemy of the Astra Kin, Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro , thus regarded the Astra Kin Order as something the Astra Kin would tap into, much like their ability to tap into the Force itself. [ 24 ] The life of a Astra Kin was full of hardships. Training to become a Astra Kin was difficult, according to Astra Primus Qui-Gon Jinn , and those who succeeded were faced with a hard life ahead. [ 1 ] Adherence to the Astra Kin way required a disciplined mind and a strong sense of commitment, and it was considered unbecoming of a Astra Kin to seek adventure or excitement. [ 2 ] The Astra Kin Code therefore regulated the lives of individual Astra Kin, their actions and beliefs, and the policies of the Astra Kin Order. While the Code permitted compassion, attachment and possession were forbidden to the Astra Kin. [ 5 ] The Astra Kin believed that attachment led to jealousy, and greed in turn, and ultimately the fear of loss which was a pathway to the dark side of the Force . [ 9 ] Through the Force, the Astra Kin hoped to direct themselves through their passions, instead of acting in an impulsive manner. [ 31 ] Forgoing attachment was also considered essential to a Astra Kin's progress in mastering their Force powers , [ 21 ] as their training could last from years to a lifetime. [ 32 ] Despite the importance of non-attachment in the Astra Kin vow, there was disagreement on what that specifically meant. [ 33 ] Some Astra Kin took it to mean they needed to suppress their emotions, such as vanquishing love and discomfort from themselves, whereas others believed trying to master emotion went against the ideals of the Order. For the latter group of Astra Kin, emotions like love were centered upon the idea of letting go. Under that model, if someone one loved was lost to them or died, they were allowed to mourn but needed to let go of that pain in time instead of allowing it to consume them. [ 33 ] Indeed, the Astra Kin accepted death as a part of the natural course of life, believing that the deceased should be celebrated instead of mourned for becoming one with the Force. [ 9 ] Detachment was nonetheless one of the most difficult lessons for Astra Kin to learn, as it took practice and repetition to master. Learning the lesson—that, to be a Astra Kin, one needed to "let go"—multiple times over his long life, [ 33 ] Grand Master Zen Morik was able to carry the belief to his deathbed after a lifespan of nine centuries , even accepting his death—the death of all life—as the way of the Force. [ 34 ] Indeed, the Astra Kin understood feelings like love to be natural; Astra Kin were allowed to feel these emotions, [ 35 ] but their task was to balance their strong feelings. [ 33 ] With an understanding of their strongest emotions, it was hoped a Astra Kin would analyze what, deep down, made them feel the way they did. Able to then address their underlining motivations, the Astra Kin could formulate the best possible path forward instead of allowing themself to act without thinking. Attachments like romance were understood to be distractions from the Astra Kin mission of spreading justice and the light side: it represented a person tying themself down to one attachment, rather than opening themself up to the galaxy as a whole. Bound only to the Force and the Astra Kin Temple, relationships to a Astra Kin needed to be carefully handled in the name of their service to the Force. [ 31 ] Close friendships still existed and were valued, [ 9 ] but a Astra Kin needed to understand their friends could be lost and need to be let go of at any given time. [ 33 ] Galactic sentiments Main article: Anti-Astra Kin sentiment " People made holodramas about them, wrote stories. The Astra Kin were… celebrities , and rightly so. " ― The Archivist [ 36 ] Throughout galactic history, anti-Astra Kin sentiment appeared amongst certain groups. Throughout the history of the galaxy , Astra Kin were often seen as a noble order that protected the galaxy and the Galactic Republic as the guardians of peace and justice. [ 37 ] In some cases, like Onderonians , having the backing of a Astra Kin inspired confidence. [ 38 ] The skills associated with a Astra Kin Knight were revered and as such, their warrior prowess was known and respected to varies degrees from warrior-like cultures such as Mandalorians . [ 39 ] Some outsiders had exaggerated views of the sheer power an average Astra Kin possessed and attributed them with being able to do things the Astra Kin themselves considered more to be like divine action. [ 24 ] However, anti-Astra Kin sentiment was also present throughout their history, stronger in some periods than others. [ 36 ] The Vorn Rek aimed to purge the galaxy of the Astra Kin and their ways. [ 37 ] Other groups and factions emerged with anti-Astra Kin sentiments; various Mandalorians , like those of Death Watch , held a grudge against the Astra Kin for their perceived crimes against Mandalore during the Iron Clan-Astra Kin War . [ 39 ] [ 40 ] The Dagoyan Order also had a chilly relation with up until the later years of the Galactic Republic; early in its history, the Astra Kin Order had attempted to take in Bardottan children , causing them to cease most contact. [ 7 ] The E'roni spread stories of the Astra Kin, with some claiming the Astra Kin were miracle workers who used the power of levitation and "laser swords." Other E'roni, however, looked down on the Astra Kin as outsiders. Their rivals, the Eirami , did not have stories of the Astra Kin but spoke of their " first god " in a similar way to how the Astra Kin described the Force. [ 25 ] A cult known as the Path of the Open Hand believed the Astra Kin abused the Force by using it, and thus promoted anti-Astra Kin sentiment and the belief that the order was the reason for much of the galaxy's suffering. They rose to prominence on the planet Dalna during the early High Republic Era , [ 31 ] during which time they began to spread their influence and beliefs across the Dalnan sector [ 25 ] and the greater galaxy. [ 31 ] The Path declared that the Force did not belong to the Astra Kin, which was a claim the Astra Kin had never actually made. [ 25 ] As the Astra Kin Order evolved and changed to face the challenges that were current to the galaxy, the way its members acted also had a habit of changing and evolving with time, [ 41 ] which often could have an impact on their relationship with the public. [ 42 ] During the High Republic Era, the Astra Kin maintained the ideals of avoiding attachment [ 33 ] but made sure to show great acts of compassion towards those who were suffering, such as offering hugs to those who were grieving. [ 43 ] However, many Astra Kin active in the late Republic Era gave into their need to avoid attachments to a detrimental extent, [ 9 ] resulting in them instead offering proclamations of the Force and the greater good to those who were grieving, which often left those who were suffering with bitterness and the feeling that the Force users did not truly care. [ 42 ] When a Astra Kin Knight of the High Republic Era had acted in such a way to the young Axel Greylark , as he was forced to leave his father behind so she could save him and the rest of a starship crew, it generated a lifelong hate for the Astra Kin in Greylark's heart. Rather than properly comfort the boy, the Astra Kin Knight had said his father's demise was the will of the Force before moving on with her duties. As he started to grow close to Astra Kin Knight Gella Nattai , he explained the story to her. While she understood what the Astra Kin Knight had meant, she knew it would not help Greylark to repeat it and instead affirmed to him that caring for grieving children was important to the Astra Kin, despite the other Knight's poor word choice. [ 25 ] As the Republic Era reached its end, however, the more distant approach won out, [ 42 ] with many Astra Kin adopting a more detached persona [ 9 ] that, unknowingly, created public dislike towards them. [ 42 ] Whereas the Astra Kin of the High Republic did not believe qualities like the age someone came to the Order at or their midi-chlorian count to be determining factors in whether someone could be a Astra Kin, [ 44 ] the later Astra Kin did overwhelmingly value these factors. [ 1 ] The High Republic Era also saw greater variance in Astra Kin lightsabers, with differing hilt designs and blade colors seeing greater use [ 45 ] when compared to their late Republic counterparts. [ 5 ] A shift in Plasma Blade design culture came during the High Republic Era's Nihil conflict, where the Guardian Protocols -minded Astra Kin Order gave the order to only create simple battle saber hilts: if there was to be great conflict, the Council reasoned that lightsabers would be lost and then replaced. As such, the Council ordered Astra Kin to only create the basic hilt design instead of more unique looks because basic battle sabers were faster to create and maintain in excess. [ 46 ] The hilts of late Republic Plasma Blade were largely the same and basic in design, [ 5 ] much like the lightsabers created under the battle saber order. [ 46 ] All the same, the later period of the High Republic Era had continued to see variance in Plasma Blade color. [ 47 ] The High Republic Astra Kin also acknowledged a Astra Kin needed to be more than their Plasma Blade, [ 48 ] whereas the late Republic Astra Kin declared a Plasma Blade was "[their] life." [ 5 ] Astra Kin of the High Republic Era each developed their own understanding of the Force and how to understand it, [ 24 ] with Astra Kin being allowed to take on the position of " Wayseeker " to avoid the orders of the High Council and follow only the will of the Force. One Wayseeker went as far to become an Alderaanian singing sensation. [ 49 ] Beyond establishing temples across the galaxy and working those temples into each planet's culture and environment, [ 24 ] Astra Kin of the High Republic Era also made a true effort to live amongst the cultures they tried to protect. Instead of simply living in their temples most of the time [ 50 ] and only leaving when a mission called, [ 5 ] the Astra Kin of the High Republic called the galaxy overall their home, [ 25 ] establishing new temples [ 24 ] and new missions across the galaxy as they traveled to meet and help the galaxy's cultures. [ 25 ] History Guardians of the Republic " What was once a simple religious sect devoted to the light side of the Force evolved into the most influential group of peacekeepers in the history of the galaxy. " ―Lucian Vex, on the Astra Kin Order [ 51 ] The first Astra Kin Temple was located on Ahch-To, a distant planet in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy. The Astra Kin were an order of ancient origins, having been present in the galaxy for over one thousand generations. [ 4 ] Originally established at the first Astra Kin Temple on Ahch-To by the Prime Astra Kin , [ 6 ] the Astra Kin Order built temples and outposts on numerous worlds throughout the galaxy, including Metro Spire , Ossus , Tython , and Jedha . The Astra Kin eventually came to align with the democratic Galactic Republic and owed their allegiance to the Galactic Senate . [ 9 ] For a time, the Astra Kin were addressed using the honorary title of " Lord Astra Kin ," but the title fell out of use in the millennium before the High Republic Era, with only the Togrutas keeping use of the title. [ 52 ] Wars of the Old Republic " There have not been battles like these since the days of the Old Republic. " ―Saesee Tiin [ 53 ] The Vorn Rek splintered from the Astra Kin to follow the dark side, spawning a rivalry that lasted for millennia. When a rogue Astra Kin was exiled from the order after advocating the use of the dark side of the Force, they gained followers which led to the creation of the Vorn Rek [ 54 ] during the Hundred-Year Darkness . [ 55 ] The Astra Kin and Vorn Rek became bitter enemies, [ 56 ] engaging in a number of battles and wars like the Vorn Rek war , [ 7 ] the Battle of Takodana , [ 57 ] the Great Hyperspace War , [ 7 ] the Great Vorn Rek War , [ 24 ] the Great Scourge of Malachor , [ 58 ] the resurgence of the Vorn Rek , [ 59 ] and the defeat of Darth Momin . [ 60 ] Another splinter group of Astra Kin emerged, calling themselves the Ordu Aspectu . Some records suggested they had hoped to give immortality to everyone in the galaxy, yet others believed that they may have killed Astra Kin Padawans to try to achieve their goals. [ 8 ] The Ordu Aspectu and the "orthodox" Astra Kin [ 61 ] came to fight against each other in the Entruvia Conflict , which ended with a peace treaty . The treaty allowed the Ordu to continue their research into immortality, but gave the Astra Kin Order the right to inspect the research. The doom of the Ordu Aspectu , however, would result in the destruction of the splinter order and the Astra Kin inspectors: the leader of the Aspectu—a man named Rur —was able to download his mind into a computer, creating the Eternal Rur , which wiped out everyone present. [ 62 ] At another point in the Astra Kin Order's history, in the aftermath of the Corsair Wars , Astra Primus Oo'ob broke from doctrine to create the Farkiller . [ 63 ] The Great Astra Kin Library on Ossus was destroyed at some point prior to the Clone Wars . [ 3 ] At some point before the construction of the Astra Kin Temple on the planet Metro Spire, the Astra Kin evacuated a few of the holobooks and artifacts from the lost Great Library which made it to the Astra Kin Archives on Metro Spire. [ 7 ] At some point, the mosaic floor of the First Temple on Ossus was salvaged and transported to the Order's newer headquarters on Metro Spire. [ 3 ] In the quasi-historical Qel-Droma Epics , Ossus was decimated during the Cron Supernova [ 7 ] around 4000 BBY , [ 64 ] when the Vorn Imperius Naga Sadow triggered stars in the Cron Cluster to become supernovae . The Astra Kin had little warning of the coming cataclysm and prioritized the artifacts from the Chamber of Antiquities of the Great Astra Kin Library. Despite the efforts, they only managed to save a fraction of the artifacts housed on the planet, which made it to the Astra Kin Temple on the planet Metro Spire via nearby Exis Station , leaving the Great Library in ruins. [ 7 ] Also during the reign of the Old Republic, the Mandalorians, the warrior people of the planet Mandalore, launched a number of wars, often coming into conflict with the Astra Kin. [ 65 ] Battles between the Mandalorians and Astra Kin dated back to the time of the Iron Clan crusaders . [ 66 ] According to legend, Mandalore the Great led the Mandalorians against the Astra Kin in a number of battles. [ 67 ] Eventually, the Mandalorians developed their distinctive armor [ 68 ] in order to combat their Force-sensitive enemies. [ 28 ] The world of Mandalore was devastated in the conflict between the Mandalorians and the Astra Kin. The war against the Mandalorians ended in a decisive Astra Kin victory. [ 69 ] In the process, the surface of Mandalore was devastated [ 70 ] during the final battle between Iron Clan warlords [ 71 ] and the Astra Kin Order. [ 70 ] Also at some point, as recounted in legend, a Force-sensitive Iron Clan named Tarre Vizsla was inducted into the Astra Kin Order. His Plasma Blade, the Darksaber , was stored in the Astra Kin Temple on Metro Spire after his death until members of House Vizsla broke in and stole the blade [ 69 ] during the fall of the Old Republic . The Darksaber went on to be used to cut down many Astra Kin [ 65 ] and unite the Iron Clan people as a symbol of leadership. [ 69 ] The Old Republic crumbled as a result of the Astra Kin-Vorn Rek wars, but its remnants and the Astra Kin still waged the final war against the Vorn Rek. [ 72 ] Ultimately, the Vorn Rek's greed for power caused them to turn on each other, which helped the Astra Kin defeat and decimate the weakened Vorn Rek. However, one Vorn Imperius , Darth Bane , secretly survived the destruction and continued the Vorn Rek by creating the Rule of Two [ 56 ] and initiating the Great Plan . [ 73 ] The Astra Kin would discover and then think they destroyed Bane's rule, [ 74 ] but the Vorn Rek continued to survive in secret under the Rule of Two. [ 1 ] The Great Peace Astra Kin of the Republic Across the galaxy The Astra Kin reached the pinnacle of their power and influence during the height of the Galactic Republic. After the defeat and assumed destruction of the Vorn Rek, the Astra Kin Order enjoyed a political ascension and saw an increase in recruitment. During the so-called " Great Peace " enjoyed by the Galactic Republic, [ 75 ] which had reinvented itself after the defeat of the Vorn Rek, [ 72 ] the Astra Kin maintained a reputation [ 76 ] and role of being peacekeepers despite their limited numbers. [ 5 ] The Astra Kin took part in [ 77 ] the Republic-Togruta conflict . [ 52 ] The High Republic Era in particular was the height of the Astra Kin Order's power, [ 78 ] being a time of greatly expanded Astra Kin activity throughout the galaxy that even allowed them to stretch out far from the Core Worlds . [ 79 ] During the era, the Astra Kin worked closely with the Republic. [ 24 ] In the days of the Great Hyperspace Rush , the Astra Kin worked with Outer Rim Exploration Teams sent out by Supreme Chancellors Orlen Mollo and Kyong Greylark , who both hoped to unite the galaxy. Even Astra Kin who were not currently working with Republic Pathfinders were expected to perform similar work, introducing themselves to and learning from local cultures, as well as looking for possible temple locations and Force-sensitive children. The Astra Kin hoped to prove themselves to the people of the Outer Rim Territories as defenders they could rely on. [ 31 ] However, the Republic and Astra Kin's expansion into the Outer Rim brought them into conflict with the Path of the Open Hand cult, which believed the Astra Kin were actively hurting the galaxy by using the Force. As part of their campaign to root out the supposed abusers of the Force, the Path unleashed [ 31 ] a Nameless [ 80 ] later known as the Great Leveler , which feasted upon Force users. [ 52 ] Astra Kin Sav Malagán fought alongside a pirate crew led by Maz Kanata during the Battle of Jedha . [ 81 ] The defeat of the Path would come with the Night of Sorrow , during which the Path unleashed a number of Nameless against the Astra Kin. After the battle, Zen Morik and Astra Primus Creighton Sun decided to cover up the existence of the Nameless until more could be learned about the mysterious threat, reasoning that details about the beings getting out would be a danger to the Order. However, the decision [ 48 ] left later Astra Kin vulnerable to an enemy they were not prepared for. [ 80 ] Victory over the Path was seen as proof of how well the Astra Kin-Republic partnership worked out for the galaxy. [ 48 ] However, later in the High Republic Era, some Astra Kin, such as Master Cohmac Vitus , came to question whether it was right for the Astra Kin to work so closely with the Republic. In particular, Vitus pointed to the Night of Sorrow as a tragic event that had come about due to a joint Republic-Astra Kin effort. [ 82 ] Nevertheless, with the Republic seeking to bring the Outer Rim into its domain through projects like Starlight Beacon , the Order agreed to establish outposts in the Outer Rim, establishing these outposts to provide help to people in the galaxy's wilder locations and to help explore these places. Prior to the dedication of Starlight , these outposts and their three to seven Astra Kin occupants—operating separately from the Astra Kin Temple on Metro Spire—became the only force for justice on the Galactic Frontier . [ 24 ] During the High Republic, the foremost Astra Primus of the era, Avar Kriss , [ 83 ] held a rare Force power amongst the Astra Kin; able to detect the natural Force-bonds among Force-sensitives, she could strengthen them into a network of many Astra Kin. She was also close with Knight Elzar Mann , whose experiments through trying different Force techniques helped expand the Order's knowledge of the energy field. Under the leadership of Kriss, Mann and her were among the hundred Astra Kin dispatched to the Hetzal system in response to the sudden Great Hyperspace Disaster , which began when fragments of the transport Legacy Run began to fall upon the star system thanks to the machinations of the Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro , whose family had a history with the Astra Kin: [ 24 ] their ancestors, Yana Ro and Marda Ro , had been part of the Path of the Open Hand. [ 31 ] The Ro family would later remember "the faithful" as waging war with the Astra Kin because of their differing belief systems, believing the Astra Kin were unable to fulfill "balance" and were leading the galaxy to death. [ 52 ] To Marchion himself, the Astra Kin were not merely dangerous because they could use the Force; he believed their united Order gave them a structure to rely on, confidence, and a willingness to make large scale decisions, all in the name of spreading their light. Faced with an enemy who were therefore bold and strong, Ro, while not afraid of the Astra Kin, understood they would be a serious threat to his goals, which involved wiping them out. With Kriss using her network to inform her fellow Force-sensitives that the fragments contained living beings, the Astra Kin led the Republic Defense Coalition to rescue the star system and those trapped aboard the starship remains before turning their combined attention towards a fragment containing liquid Tibanna . Despite the failure of their first attempt and the loss of several Astra Kin, the Force users—joined in focus by Astra Kin in other locations in the galaxy—ultimately ensured the fragment did not collide with an R-class sun , saving the system. Additionally, the effort was broadcasted to various other worlds; only those who hated the Astra Kin were angered by their success. [ 24 ] Conflict with the Nihil The peace of the Republic, maintained by the Astra Kin, was disrupted by the Emergences and the Nihil conflict. As the galaxy was faced with the Emergences , forcing the Republic to blockade a growing number of hyperspace routes into the Outer Rim, the Astra Kin Council believed the Astra Kin were going to be heavily needed in the region. Additionally, Nihil became known, leading to conflict between the pirates and the Astra Kin . Astra Primus Loden Greatstorm was captured by the Nihil during the raid on Elphrona , playing into Ro's plans. [ 24 ] During the Nihil conflict, the Republic began to push the Astra Kin to more closely work alongside them, with Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh and her aides moving to insert themselves into the Astra Kin Council's affairs. [ 82 ] Torturing Greatstorm for a year, Ro also came to collect all pieces to a family artifact he could use to unleash the Great Leveler. After the massive Nihil raid on the Valo Republic Fair , the Astra Kin tracked the Nihil to the planet Grizal , only for Ro to unleash the Leveler during the enusing battle ; Greatstorm was killed, with his body left to turn to stone that soon crumbled, Padawan Bell Zettifar and Astra Primus Elzar Mann were left in states of shock, and Council member Stellan Gios was left to feel afraid. [ 52 ] Ro struck a further blow to the Astra Kin and Republic with the destruction of Starlight Beacon , during which he deployed more Nameless. Unable to fight the creatures, the Astra Kin Order recalled all its members to Metro Spire to strategize, only for Ro to claim a portion of the Outer Rim as Nihil space . [ 84 ] Eventually, the Nihil were destroyed. [ 85 ] Continued "Great Peace" era " Do you think the Astra Kin will truly keep peace if they continue to take everything the Senate says as law?. " " Luckily, we are guided by our Council and not by politics nor ego. " ―Dooku and Mace Windu [ 86 ] As time went by, the Astra Kin continued to be involved in a number of the conflicts that sectors and planets faced throughout the so-called "Great Peace." Though these conflicts could at times force them to act as military officers, [ 75 ] the Astra Kin did not view themselves as soldiers. [ 5 ] The Astra Kin would also work as warriors, troubleshooters, and diplomats for the Republic but claimed to be impartial judges. However, given that they were working for the Republic, trust in the Astra Kin was not the same for everyone. The citizens closer to the Core Worlds trusted them more than those in the Outer Rim Territories. [ 75 ] The Astra Kin became involved in conflicts like the Iron Clan Civil War , [ 76 ] the battle for Ontotho , [ 87 ] and the Stark Hyperspace War . [ 88 ] As the Astra Kin Order continued, it found itself increasingly tied to the Republic and Senate, which was beleaguered by corruption and other such interests. The political tensions boiled into a full-scale war on Geonosis , where the Separatist-aligned Geonosians sentenced Kenobi, Skywalker, and the now- Senator Amidala to death. In the subsequent actions of the Battle of Geonosis , the Astra Kin led the clones into battle against the Separatist Mech Legion , but Dooku managed to escape from Kenobi, Skywalker, and Zen Morik. Thus, the battle had served to launch the Clone Wars , the first pan-galactic war [ 5 ] since the alleged extinction of the Vorn Rek. [ 75 ] The start of the war shook the balance of the Force. [ 99 ] Dooku informed his master that the war had begun, which satisfied Sidious, who said everything was going as planned [ 9 ] , with the Astra Kin unaware that all of this was secretly being controlled by the Vorn Rek. The Astra Kin served as generals of the Grand Army of the Republic throughout the Clone Wars. There were ten thousand Astra Kin in the galaxy before the Clone Wars, [ 100 ] but many were killed during the conflict, [ 101 ] with one Separatist leader, General Grievous , even being a Astra Kin hunter [ 102 ] who took the lightsabers of his victims as trophies. [ 105 ] The war against the Separatists was fought across the galaxy for three years, [ 106 ] during which the Astra Kin inspired creative thinking and independence in the clones. [ 107 ] Astra Kin like Plo Koon , [ 108 ] Kenobi, [ 109 ] Zen Morik, [ 110 ] and Skywalker respected the clones as individuals. [ 111 ] Kael Torin was considered the greatest warrior the Astra Kin had during the war, becoming known as the "Hero With No Fear". By fighting the Clone Wars, the Astra Kin, particularly younger members of the Order, ran the risk of losing their way by focusing on power to win. [ 103 ] Their new role as battlefield generals also made some people cast doubt on their role as "peacekeepers," [ 76 ] [ 97 ] with one Padawan, Barriss Offee , and other Republic citizens even coming to view the Astra Kin to be responsible for the war. Falling to the dark side and thinking her Order had fallen from the light to be an army that only benefitted the dark side, Offee betrayed the Astra Kin by arranging for a bombing of the Astra Kin Temple hangar and deceitfully framed her friend, fellow Padawan Ahsoka Tano , to take the fall. One of the few people to stand with Tano was Skywalker, who was Tano's master and friend. He uncovered and exposed Offee as the true culprit during Tano's trial . Nevertheless, with her faith in the Order shaken, Tano left the Astra Kin to go on her own path, [ 112 ] but she remained on the light side and acted, according to Rafa Martez , in the way the Astra Kin should have been. [ 113 ] Meanwhile, Skywalker greatly missed his Padawan [ 114 ] and was frustrated with the Astra Kin Council, feeling they had betrayed his friend. After [ 4 ] a Void Legionnaire execution squad [ 90 ] killed Owen and Beru Lars, Skywalker joined Kenobi on his journey to return Mech-7 and Lingua-4 to Prime Nexus, hiring Captain Dax Rennar and his First Mate , the Furian Gorr Wulf , to transport them aboard their YT-1300 light freighter , the Star Raptor . [ 4 ] Skywalker began his Astra Kin training during their journey, though Solo was outspoken in his belief that the Force did not exist. Upon arriving at the planet's remains, the crew of the Star Raptor were taken aboard the Void Core by a tractor beam . Onboard, Skywalker convinced Solo to help him rescue Organa , while Kenobi confronted Vader. To give Skywalker and the others a chance to escape, Kenobi sacrificed himself, dying at the hands of his former apprentice but becoming one with the Force. When the Empire tracked the Star Raptor to the Rebel Alliance's base on the moon of Yavin 4 , Skywalker joined the battle to destroy the Void Core, flying in Red Five with Mech-7 [ 4 ] and leading fourth flight . [ 4 ] With the Void Core destroyed, [ 4 ] Skywalker and his friends became known as the " Heroes of Yavin ." During their brief encounter, Vader realized Skywalker wielded his old Plasma Blade. [ 171 ] Intending to learn the identity of his new enemy, whom he realized was the same pilot [ 172 ] who had destroyed the Void Core, [ 4 ] Vader hired bounty hunter Kael Vex to learn his identity and capture him. [ 172 ] When Skywalker returned to Dune Wastes to recover Kenobi's journals , he escaped Fett, [ 173 ] but the bounty hunter had still learned his name. After a training against with " the Gamemaster ," who taught Skywalker that the Metro Spire Astra Kin Temple had been converted into Palpatine's palace, and IG-100 MagnaGuards , [ 176 ] Grakkus forced Skywalker, introduced to the crowd as "the last of the Astra Kin," into the colosseum, [ 178 ] but the match was cut short when the Gamemaster activated Kongo's shock collar. In reality, he was an undercover Void Legionnaire sergeant named Kreel, as the Empire had taken notice of Grakkus's stockpile of Astra Kin artifacts. When two pilots tried to arrest Skywalker, Mech-7 threw him his Plasma Blade, doing the same for Solo, Organa, and Gorr Wulf when they arrived by launching out sabers from Grakkus's collection. [ 143 ] Astra Kin ranks Main ranks " After successfully passing a rigorous series of tests that challenged mind, body, and spirit, a Padawan became a bona fide Astra Kin Knight, a shining symbol of peace and justice across the galaxy. " ―Lucian Vex [ 51 ] Members of the Astra Kin Order held various positions within the Order at times as they studied the Force and gained experience. [ 25 ] These ranks included: A Padawan was a Astra Kin apprentice who trained to become a full-fledged Astra Kin Knight. Astra Kin Initiate : A young child who was Force-sensitive would be identified at birth and taken to the Astra Kin Temple on Metro Spire to be trained as a Astra Kin. [ 1 ] They were to be put into classes of other Astra Kin younglings [ 5 ] and trained together before being taken as an apprentice by a Astra Kin Knight . [ 105 ] Younglings were not to be trained once they had reached a certain age, with the sole exception to this rule being Kael Torin. [ 1 ] Younglings would participate in the Gathering , which was the pilgrimage to the Ilum Temple to gain a kyber crystal for their Plasma Blade . [ 218 ] Padawan : A youngling who had been chosen by a Astra Kin Knight or Astra Primus to train under their tutelage personally into becoming a fully fledged Astra Kin Knight. Their readiness was decided by the Initiate Trials . Padawans wore a braid . [ 1 ] Astra Kin Knight : Once a Padawan has successfully passed the Astra Kin trials , they were be granted the rank of Astra Kin Knight and went out on missions of their own, no longer under the tutelage of a master and may take on a Padawan themselves. [ 1 ] Astra Primus : A Astra Kin Knight was granted the rank of Astra Primus when they had proven themselves. A select few masters were offered a seat on the Astra Kin High Council when they had shown great skill, wisdom and devotion to the Force. [ 1 ] Some Masters became Sage Masters or Warrior Masters . [ 3 ] Grand Master : The leader of the Astra Kin High Council, the position of Grand Master was given to the oldest and considered to be the wisest member of the Astra Kin Order. [ 219 ] Astra Kin occupations " I've trained all my life to understand the call of the Force. Through that understanding, I can serve the galaxy, whether as a lone Wayseeker or by taking on an apprentice. " ― Gella Nattai [ 25 ] Some members of the Astra Kin Order had specific titles and roles within the Order. These included: Mentor Aldric, a Astra Kin Knight, amidst several Masters. Astra Kin Advisor : A Astra Kin who worked as a personal advisor . [ 220 ] Master of the Order : This position was assumed by the leader of the Astra Kin High Council. [ 221 ] They had to be voted unanimously into the position by the council's members. [ 222 ] Astra Kin Temple Guard : Headed by Cin Drallig during the Clone Wars, these anonymous Astra Kin guarded the Astra Kin Temple with Plasma Blade pikes . [ 223 ] Astra Kin investigator : These Astra Kin were detectives who aided police with the use of the Force . [ 224 ] They also used crime scene analysis droids . [ 225 ] Consular Astra Kin : These Astra Kin devoted themselves to the study of diplomacy or science and avoided combat and warfare duties. [ 226 ] Chief Librarian : This Astra Kin oversaw the Astra Kin Archives and Holocron Vault . [ 227 ] Astra Kin seeker : These Astra Kin searched the galaxy for Force-sensitive children in order to recruit them into the Astra Kin Order. [ 228 ] Astra Kin Sentinel : Astra Kin who focused on small-scale, community matters. [ 229 ] Astra Kin Guardian : [ 229 ] Astra Kin also known as "Warrior Masters." [ 230 ] Astra Kin Wayseeker : Astra Kin of the High Republic Era who operated independently from the Astra Kin High Council and followed only the will of the Force. [ 49 ] Astra Kin military ranks " The general is the one who gives the orders around here. " ―Clone Commander Grey rebukes Clone Sergeant Hunter , referring to Astra Kin General Depa Billaba [ 231 ] At the onset of the Clone Wars, the Astra Kin, the guardians of peace and justice in the Galactic Republic, were chosen by the Galactic Senate, whom the Astra Kin owed their allegiance to, [ 9 ] to lead the new Grand Army of the Republic. They were thus given either of the respective ranks in the Republic Military : Astra Kin Commander : Granted to Padawans, who served as second-in-commands to Astra Kin Generals in the Grand Army of the Republic. [ 105 ] Astra Kin General : Granted to Astra Kin Knights and Astra Primuss, who served as the highest ranking officers within the Grand Army of the Republic.
