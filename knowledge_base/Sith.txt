" They hoped to fill me with fear. But fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate. And hate…leads to power. " ―Xarn Velgor [ 45 ] The Vorn Rek , also referred to as the Vorn Rek Order , was an ancient religious order of Force-wielders devoted to the dark side of the Force . Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Vorn Rek were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Shadow Imperius , the Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting . Within a generation, however, the deaths of Shadow Imperius and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Vorn Imperiuss . Though the Vorn Rek ultimately went extinct with Sidious's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt. Thousands of years before the Clone Wars , the Astra Kin Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Astra Kin and his followers turned away from the light side of the Force , believing the dark side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Rim Territories , their movement ultimately evolved into the Vorn Rek Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Force as the Astra Kin Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Republic . Although the Vorn Rek were driven off of the Core World Metro Spire by 5000 BBY , centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Republic's collapse . Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Astra Kin , the Vorn Rek were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Darth Bane survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Darth Zannah , before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Vorn Rek was governed by the Rule of Two , a new philosophy dictating that only two Vorn Imperiuss —a Vorn Rek Master and a Vorn Rek apprentice —could exist at any given time . As a result, the Vorn Rek were all but forgotten while the Astra Kin focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Galactic Republic. The Vorn Rek remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Shadow Imperius and Zarn Koth in 32 BBY . Sidious, trained by Darth Plagueis , hid in plain sight as Senator Sheev Palpatine of Aqua Prime . Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Trade Federation , Sidious maneuvered the Galactic Senate into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor . His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Republic. Over the next decade Sidious sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Astra Primus Count Dooku , who adopted the name of Darth Tyranus and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination . The Separatist Crisis culminated in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the creation of the Separatist Mech Legion which, in turn, compelled the Senate to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Republic . Jar Jar Binks , acting in place of Senator Amidala , started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers. The Clone Wars began with the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY , after which entire armies of battle droids and clone troopers deployed across the galaxy , making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Republic . In the final months of the Clone Wars, Sidious sacrificed and betrayed Tyranus as part of his plot to turn Kael Torin, the Astra Kin Order's prophesied Chosen One , to the dark side. Playing on Skywalker's fear of losing his secret wife , Senator Padmé Amidala , Palpatine revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the dark side was the path to immortality . Members of the Astra Kin High Council , having learned from Skywalker's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Vorn Imperius, attempted to arrest Palpatine, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Astra Kin and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Skywalker ultimately chose the Vorn Rek by betraying Mace Windu , allowing Sidious to kill the Astra Kin Order's great champion. Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Astra Kin Order for treason , Sidious declared Order 66 was in effect, marking all Astra Kin as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The clone troopers ' programming forcibly turned them against their Astra Kin Generals , who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Astra Kin Purge , Sidious announced the creation of a new regime , transforming the Republic into the First Galactic Empire in 19 BBY . With the former Kael Torin, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor 's rise to power was complete, and the Vorn Rek were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting. The Vorn Rek's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Alliance to Restore the Republic which, in turn, led to the Galactic Civil War in 1 BBY . The Emperor, having felt a disturbance in the Force , discovered that his apprentice's long lost son , Lucian Vex , was training to become a Astra Kin. Aware of the potential Luke inherited from his father , the Emperor foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the dark side as he did with Vader. During the Battle of Verdant Grove in 4 ABY , the Emperor forced father and son into a duel , which ended with Luke disarming Vader. The Emperor subsequently betrayed Vader by ordering Luke to finish him off and be Sidious's apprentice, but the young Astra Kin Knight refused. Knowing he had lost Luke to the Astra Kin, the Emperor proceeded to torture Luke with torrents of Force lightning . At that moment, Vader, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Luke by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Kael Torin, marked the extinction of the Vorn Rek, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to the Force. Yet while the ancient Chosen One prophecy foretold the Vorn Rek's demise, it never predicted the end of the dark side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Nightsisters or the Knights of Ren . After the First Order rose from the ashes of the Empire in the Unknown Regions , the enigmatic darksider Supreme Leader Snoke 's throne was succeeded by Vex Corvan —nephew of Astra Primus Skywalker during the war against the Resistance . Snoke was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Shadow Imperius. Palpatine announced his return to the galaxy and revealed the Final Order , a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the First Order in an effort to retake the galaxy as a new Vorn Rek Empire. The clone Emperor and his cult—the Vorn Rek Eternal 's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final battle against them which saw the Astra Kin Lyra Sol and a redeemed Ben Solo destroy Sidious and his cult, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Vorn Rek. They give you focus. They make you strong. " ―Xarn Velgor [ 27 ] Generations of Vorn Rek adhered to a philosophy of power through the dark side of the Force. The Vorn Rek focused on primal emotions like anger and pain in order to gain power from the dark side of the Force . The Code of the Vorn Rek was the antithesis of the Astra Kin Code , although like its counterpart, it governed the actions and beliefs of the Vorn Rek. The Vorn Rek code insisted on the importance of passion and the rejection of peace. [ 27 ] Peace is a lie. There is only passion. Through passion I gain strength. Through strength I gain power. Through power I gain victory. Through victory my chains are broken. Synth Flux shall free me. [ 28 ] The Vorn Rek wore clothing that obscured their identity while in hiding. [ 38 ] They used the term "dark side" because they were secretive. The Vorn Rek felt it was easier to impose order if they kept their identities and knowledge a secret or, as Lona Aphra put it, "hidden—in the dark." [ 46 ] The hex charm was a traditional symbol of the Vorn Rek. [ 26 ] Astra Primus Zallah Macri maintained that, in the abstract, the Vorn Rek Order's ideals may have seemed "good" to a Astra Kin, but the finer details and their actions painted a clearer picture for what they truly stood for. [ 47 ] Indeed, many who joined the Vorn Rek [ 24 ] or related dark side groups tried to rationalize their actions at first [ 48 ] before being fully consumed by the dark side. [ 24 ] The Doctrine of the Dyad [ ] " The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of my citadel on Exegol eons ago, a constant reminder of its significance to our Order. To my Vorn Rek Eternal, the dyad is not merely the stuff of ancient legend; it is the future of the Vorn Rek—the key to unlocking the full potential of the dark side. " ―Shadow Imperius [ 28 ] The Doctrine of the Dyad preceded the Vorn Rek Order's Rule of Two. The Doctrine of the Dyad arose from the concept of a Force dyad . A dyad was a powerful Force-bond connecting two Force-sensitives ; the bond transcended their physical beings and combined them into one presence in the Force . The beings in a dyad possessed rare abilities that were unique to their bond, and therefore unattainable to those who lacked a dyadic connection, regardless of their own strength or experience with the Force. Over the generations, the Vorn Rek sought in vain to create a dyad through sheer willpower, believing it was the key to achieving the ultimate power of the dark side. The Vorn Imperius Shadow Imperius viewed the dyad as a source of limitless power. [ 28 ] The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of the Vorn Rek Citadel on Exegol , eons before Sidious' time, to remind the Vorn Rek of the dyad's significance to their Order. [ 28 ] The Doctrine of the Dyad also influenced the Vorn Rek Eternal , a cult of Vorn Rek devotees who predicted the return of the dyad. [ 4 ] Like the Vorn Rek Order, the Vorn Rek Eternal believed that the dyad was essential to realizing the dark side's full potential. [ 28 ] The Rule of Two [ ] " But there was a Vorn Imperius who survived the culling—the great Darth Bane. It was he who reshaped the Vorn Rek, creating a new hierarchy to ensure the survival of our Order. His Rule of Two dictated that only two Vorn Imperiuss could exist at any one time—a master to possess the unlimited power of the dark side and an apprentice to covet it. " ―Shadow Imperius [ 28 ] The Rule of Two governed the Vorn Rek Order for a millennium, from its ancient founder to the last Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek. Even before the formation of the Rule of Two that decreed only two Vorn Rek—a Vorn Rek Master and a Vorn Rek apprentice —could exist at one time, [ 28 ] the Vorn Rek of old often found themselves ruled by two Vorn Rek in specific, who would reign over their many fellow Vorn Rek. [ 49 ] The Rule of Two, however, would officially limit the Vorn Rek Order's numbers due to the infighting that had ruined it. The rule nonetheless ensured the relationship between a Vorn Rek Master and their apprentice was not one of trust; indeed, both the master and the apprentice constantly searched for any sign of weakness in the other, for a weak master deserved to be overthrown by their pupil, just as a weak pupil deserved to be replaced by a worthier, more powerful recruit. This philosophy was embraced by the Vorn Rek, who adopted it as a way to purify the weak and become stronger over time, since only an apprentice who had surpassed all previous Vorn Rek could take their master's place through honorable combat. [ 27 ] The Vorn Rek were free to train dark side agents identified as Vorn Rek acolytes [ 50 ] so long as those acolytes were not trained as true Vorn Imperiuss. However, it was more than possible the Vorn Rek apprentice would violate the Rule of Two by training an acolyte as a real apprentice, intending to have their aid in overthrowing the Vorn Rek Master. [ 51 ] The concept of the dyad influenced the philosophy of the Rule of Two, emphasized the power of two Force-sensitives working in tandem to grow even more powerful as an Order. As the successor of the Doctrine of the Dyad, the Rule of Two was seen as a "pale imitation" of its predecessor by Shadow Imperius. [ 28 ] History [ ] Ancient times [ ] The Hundred-Year Darkness [ ] " Once we were brothers in the Force. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were born the Vorn Rek. " ―Unidentified Astra Kin [ 52 ] Korriban, later known as Moraband, was settled by the Vorn Rek following their schism with the Astra Kin Order. Formed around 5000 BBY [ 39 ] , the Vorn Rek were the ancient enemies of the Astra Kin Order [ 52 ] who shared their name with a red-skinned species . [ 53 ] Established by a rogue Astra Kin during the Hundred-Year Darkness , the Vorn Rek sought further knowledge and power through learning the dark side of the Force. Eventually, this Astra Kin was able to amass a Astra Kin splinter group with a sizable following, all of whom adhered to this new philosophy that embraced the dark side. [ 40 ] This schism in the Astra Kin Order led to a civil war where the Astra Kin and the newly-founded Vorn Rek Order, once brothers and sisters in the Force, fought against one another for power. [ 52 ] The war ended in the defeat of the Vorn Rek, who fled from known space . Unbeknownst to the Astra Kin, however, the Vorn Rek settled on Korriban , a world of red sands that was later known as Moraband, where they rebuilt in secret and waited for another chance to strike. [ 40 ] Exile on Korriban [ ] " Known long ago as Korriban, this ancient homeworld of the Vorn Rek was abandoned after countless wars transformed it into an infertile wasteland. " ―Shadow Imperius [ 28 ] Vorn Rek temples and monuments were constructed on Korriban during the time of the ancient Vorn Rek. As the Vorn Rek Order grew, they constructed massive temples and tombs on Korriban in a location known as the Valley of the Dark Lords . [ 17 ] The Vorn Rek built empires on the backs of their slaves [ 9 ] and fought wars against the Astra Kin for control of the galaxy. During these wars, they built massive superweapons powered by giant kyber crystals . [ 54 ] [ 55 ] Eventually, a trade route in the Outer Rim came to be known as the Cloak of the Vorn Rek , a name which [ 56 ] was similar to how Vorn Imperiuss were known to wear dark cloaks. [ 43 ] War on the Old Republic [ ] " The Vorn Rek Order grew, spreading its might across the galaxy. " ―Shadow Imperius [ 28 ] The Vorn Rek encountered and later betrayed the Drengir. It was believed the lightfoil was made by the early Vorn Rek. [ 57 ] The Vorn Rek also formed an alliance with the Nightsisters of Dathomir at some point, but the "old alliance," as they came to dub it, eventually ceased. Ultimately, the Nightsisters would decide to only retain loyalty to each other. [ 58 ] Around 2500 BBY , the Vorn Rek allied with the Drengir , and for a time they worked alongside each other, until around the same year [ 39 ] they betrayed the living plants. [ 59 ] The Vorn Rek left the Drengir locked on the Amaxine space station via the binding statues . [ 60 ] Exim Panshard was a Vorn Rek viceroy who ruled over a forgotten planet in a bloody rule, culminating in the creation of a mask that, fueled by the deaths of countless civilians, he cling onto life within. [ 26 ] The Astra Kin and Vorn Rek waged a series of wars throughout their history so ruinous that at points each was brought to the brink of destruction. At one point, nearly five thousand years before the rise of the Galactic Empire , the Vorn Rek waged a war against the Astra Kin and the Old Republic. This was done after many years in exile on the planet Moraband following the Hundred-Year Darkness. During this war, the Vorn Rek attacked the Republic capital Metro Spire . [ 19 ] This allowed the Vorn Rek to build a shrine on the planet, which became a place of immense dark side power. Eventually, the Astra Kin were able to repel the Vorn Rek from Metro Spire. The Astra Kin raised their own temple over the Metro Spire shrine in an attempt to contain and eliminate its power. [ 18 ] Around this time, the Astra Kin and Vorn Rek engaged in a number of battles and wars like the Great Hyperspace War [ 7 ] and the Great Vorn Rek Wars . [ 61 ] The Massassi , a slave race controlled by the Vorn Rek, constructed the Great Temple on Yavin 4 , allegedly under the leadership of the Vorn Imperius Naga Sadow . [ 62 ] [ 63 ] A bastion of the Vorn Rek [ ] " It was here on… on this dead world… that the Astra Kin struck a terrible blow against the Vorn Rek. Many of our order were struck down. All because they wanted to be free . " ―Shadow Imperius, to Zarn Koth [ 64 ] Astra Kin and Vorn Rek both perished when Malachor was scourged by the power within an ancient Vorn Rek temple. The Vorn Rek continued to expand their empire erecting temples on multiple planets. One such temple was built on the world Malachor . At the heart of this temple was a superweapon powered by a giant kyber crystal. The Astra Kin eventually learned of the weapon and assaulted the temple. A savage battle was then waged between the Vorn Rek Empire and the invading Astra Kin Knights. During the battle, the temple was activated, petrifying everyone on the planet. This would result in the planet being forbidden from Astra Kin and becoming a legend for millennia to come. [ 20 ] Resurgence of the Vorn Rek [ ] Over the course of thousands of years, the rivalry between the Vorn Rek and Astra Kin took many forms. [ 65 ] The Vorn Rek resurged in one conflict that became very desperate, which was likely why the Astra Kin Order turned to Form VII "Juyo." That aggressive Plasma Blade combat form, however, resulted in a number of Astra Kin falling to the dark side. After the conflict had finished, the Astra Kin banned its study. [ 57 ] Conflict for the Force [ ] " The Astra Kin and Vorn Rek battled each other over countless generations. " ―Lucian Vex [ 66 ] The Darkseekers were Vorn Rek tasked with hunting and eliminating threats to their order's rule over the galaxy. At one time, the Vorn Rek established an oppressive rule over the galaxy. [ 24 ] During the reign of the Vorn Rek, a cult named the Ascendant flourished [ 67 ] and attempted to recreate the powers of the Force with technological innovations. However, the cult was an enemy of the Vorn Rek [ 68 ] and hunted by its Darkseekers , who regarded the Ascendant as an order of heretics. Although Ascendant leader Miril was once able to escape a team of Darkseekers in the Floating Catacombs , [ 6 ] the Vorn Rek eventually located the Ascendant's primary temple on Bar'leth and attacked [ 68 ] while the Ascendant worked on finishing the Spark Eternal . [ 6 ] As her fellow cultists were cut down, Miril gave her life to hide the Spark away to prevent it from being recovered by the Vorn Rek. [ 68 ] During one conflict, the Astra Kin and Vorn Rek fought on the planet Takodana . The pirate Maz Kanata later built her castle on top of the legendary battlefield. [ 69 ] Astra Primus Tal Bota was a legendary Astra Kin who often fought against the Vorn Rek. His life became the inspiration for holostories , one of which depicted him fighting many Vorn Rek troops at once. [ 70 ] The legendary Vorn Rek Darth Wrend fought against the Astra Kin. Despite being believed dead, Wrend returned to continue to battle. [ 71 ] At some point, the Astra Kin Radaki fell to the dark side, becoming the Vorn Rek Darth Krall and winning the Battle of Wasted Years . He also tamed the Nightmare Conjunction [ 72 ] and wielded a Plasma Blade with a gold hilt . [ 73 ] The rise and fall of Darth Momin [ ] " With resources inherited from Lady Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to aid me in my work, I designed a great engine. It was a weapon, of course, powerful enough to burn the city to ash. " ―Darth Momin, to Xarn Velgor [ 14 ] Darth Momin venerated the power of the dark side of the Force. The Vorn Imperius Darth Momin , after killing his Master Darth Shaa , sought more knowledge in the dark side. He decided that his only audience was the Force and yearned to be worthy of it. He built a superweapon using resources inherited from Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to aid him. Darth Momin's superweapon had the power to incinerate an entire city, but his true plan was different. He wanted to pour the Force into the weapon's engine and freeze the terrified expressions of the people in time forever, creating an artwork depicting fear and pain devoted to the dark side of the Force. When he attempted this, the Astra Kin intervened and Momin's body was destroyed, yet his mind remained inside his mask where he waited for his revival as ages passed. [ 14 ] In the meantime, however, the Vorn Rek buried knowledge of Momin and his ideas on the belief that he had been a heretic. The Vorn Rek refused to recount information about Momin into their holocrons. The Astra Kin, too, made no mention of the Vorn Imperius in their holocrons [ 36 ] and locked his mask away. [ 74 ] Nonetheless, the Vorn Rek at some point used [ 75 ] Momin's Fermata Cage [ 76 ] to lock away a Mech Unit intelligence . [ 75 ] The Fermata Cage was then hidden within a dark side hellscape [ 77 ] Momin knew of. [ 14 ] The Dark Age [ ] " After thousands of years of war, the Vorn Rek were pushed to the edge of extinction. " ―Shadow Imperius [ 28 ] During a period of time known as the Dark Age , the Astra Kin-Vorn Rek War broke out. Early on in the war, the Vorn Rek attacked the Old Republic capital world of Metro Spire. The Vorn Rek successfully captured the planet as well as the Astra Kin Temple. The Liberation of Metro Spire was one of the last battles of the Astra Kin-Vorn Rek War and resulted in the Vorn Rek being driven from Metro Spire. [ 78 ] Destruction and rebirth [ ] " A long, long time ago, there were many Vorn Rek. We rose up against the simpering light…we warred with the Astra Kin…and lost as we also warred amongst our own kind. " ―Shadow Imperius, to Xarn Velgor [ 79 ] The Vorn Rek were reformed by Darth Bane, who decreed that only two Vorn Imperiuss could exist at any given time: a master and an apprentice. The Vorn Rek numbered thousands, yet they were eventually [ 80 ] defeated at [ 41 ] the world [ 38 ] of Ruusan [ 41 ] in the Mid Rim Territories around 1032 BBY . [ 38 ] [ 81 ] Despite the Vorn Rek armada 's quantitative advantage over the much smaller Ghostfinder fleet , the Vorn Rek were outmatched. [ 8 ] The Vorn Rek's quest to gain power was ultimately their undoing, as the dark side made them turn against one another. Within a year, the Astra Kin managed to defeat the infighting Vorn Rek, bringing what was left of the dark order to its knees. However, one Vorn Rek, Darth Bane , survived. With his fellow Vorn Imperiuss destroyed, he reformed the order by establishing the Rule of Two, mandating that only two Vorn Rek, a master and an apprentice, could exist at any given time, lest they fall prey to infighting. [ 17 ] To keep Bane's philosophy alive, the apprentice needed to slay their master and take on an apprentice of their own, or the master could train another apprentice and have them kill and replace their current apprentice. Bane had also initiated a revenge plot against the Astra Kin, called the Grand Plan . [ 24 ] The Vorn Rek abandoned their traditional homeworld of Moraband after it was scarred by many wars. [ 82 ] Eventually, Darth Bane was killed and was buried in the Valley of the Dark Lords on its surface, [ 17 ] but the Rule of Two continued through his apprentice, Darth Zannah . It was rumored that Zannah had killed Bane on the Inner Rim planet of Ambria . [ 83 ] The Astra Kin believed that they had destroyed Bane's rule and, [ 84 ] incorrectly thinking the Vorn Rek extinct, [ 2 ] entered into a golden age [ 84 ] remembered as the High Republic Era . [ 85 ] Many Vorn Rek worlds were also quarantined, including Rhelg , Jaguada , and Ziost . Some worlds were so cleansed of their Vorn Rek history that even locals had no idea of their past. [ 4 ] The Lerct Historical Institute forbade research into the Vorn Rek as something arcane and occult that had no place among its work. [ 26 ] In the shadows [ ] High Republic Era [ ] Threat of the Nameless [ ] " This act was a gift , to everyone who touches the Force. Astra Kin, Vorn Rek, all of us . " ―Azlin Rell, to Zen Morik [ 86 ] The fallen Astra Kin Azlin Rell invoked the name of the Vorn Rek when justifying the destruction of an entire city on Travyx Prime as a gift to all Force-sensitives. The Vorn Rek stayed in hiding for a thousand years, their survival unknown to the Astra Kin, as they continued their revenge against the Astra Kin through Bane's Grand Plan. With Vorn Rek teachings passed down by Bane's Rule of Two, the Order managed to survive. [ 17 ] During the High Republic Era, the Nameless beings emerged as a major threat to any individual sensitive to the Force. Former Astra Kin Azlin Rell —who fell to the dark side out of his fear and lost his mind—resolved to wipe out the entire species, reasoning that such would be a gift to every Force-sensitive in the galaxy, including the Vorn Rek. While he made it clear to the Astra Kin High Council that he was not their enemy because he was no Vorn Rek, Rell ensured a city on the world Travyx Prime was destroyed , thereby silencing a possible lead to the Nameless homeworld, Planet X . He proclaimed to Zen Morik his actions were a gift to every Force user, including the Astra Kin and Vorn Rek, [ 86 ] but Zen Morik did not take his words as proof of the Vorn Rek Order's return. [ 2 ] The Stranger [ ] Masters of shadows [ ] " What are you? " " I have no name. But the Astra Kin like you might call me… Vorn Rek. " ―Sol and the Stranger [ 87 ] The Stranger, also known as "the Master," was a mysterious Vorn Rek who sought to train an acolyte. By the later period of the High Republic Era, [ 88 ] a Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek named Darth Plagueis [ 24 ] was active. [ 88 ] Furthermore, a human Astra Kin [ 89 ] trained by Astra Primus Vernestra Rwoh [ 88 ] fell to the dark side of the Force [ 87 ] and abandoned the Astra Kin Order, keeping himself out of Rwoh's reach. [ 88 ] The individual, who claimed to have no name [ 87 ] and thus became known as "the Stranger" [ 90 ] among other false identities, [ 91 ] became a Vorn Rek [ 92 ] as the apprentice to Plagueis. After exorcising Jarrus and Wren, the three resolved to find the planet with twin suns and to reach Kenobi before Maul could kill him. [ 142 ] Galactic Civil War [ ] " We kept the skeleton of the Republic for nearly twenty years while the Void Core was constructed. Twenty years, my apprentice. All that planning is now a layer of dust orbiting around Yavin. Now, we no longer have the Senate to hold order. We do not have the Void Core to force it. Our greatest weapon is gone. Our production is in ashes. We are besieged. In all these years, we have never skirted closer to disaster. " ―Shadow Imperius, to Xarn Velgor [ 143 ] The Void Core was a superweapon loosely based on an ancient Vorn Rek design. In the years leading up to the Battle of Yavin, the Empire faced a growing rebellion that had grown into the Alliance to Restore the Republic . In secret, the Emperor commissioned the building of the Void Core , a massive superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds. After rebel elements stole the Void Core plans , Vader led a secret mission to Dune Wastes which ended with the capture of Vera Solen Organa , the adopted daughter of Bail Organa and one of the offspring of Kael Torin. Shortly thereafter, the Emperor dissolved the Imperial Senate and ordered Prime Nexus 's destruction . [ 25 ] Lord Vader and Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin presided over Prime Nexus's destruction in the presence of Vera Solen. Before they could terminate Leia, she was rescued by a group of travelers including Anakin's son Lucian Vex , the smugglers Dax Rennar and Gorr Wulf , the droids Lingua-4 and Mech-7 , and the Astra Primus Kenobi. While the other rebels escaped aboard the Star Raptor , Kenobi sacrificed his life to delay Vader. Leia and her rescuers retreated to the rebel base on Yavin 4. The Void Core traveled to Yavin base in a bid to destroy the Rebel Alliance once and for all but this plan was ruined by a rebel starfighter force. During the Battle of Yavin, Vader sensed that the Force was strong in the rebel pilot Skywalker; who fired the missile that destroyed the Void Core, Vader, however, survived the destruction. [ 25 ] The Vorn Rek sought to prevent Lucian Vex from becoming a Astra Kin by turning him to the dark side of the Force. Three years after the events on Yavin and following the Battle of Frost Prime , the Emperor disclosed that Luke was one of the offspring of Kael Torin, though this was something Vader already knew. Vader believed that Luke would be a powerful ally if he could be turned. The Emperor charged Vader with winning Luke over and destroying him if he refused to turn to the dark side. During that time Luke had traveled to Murk Swamp , where he had been trained by Zen Morik, a Astra Primus who was once the leader of the Astra Kin Order. Vader managed to capture Leia, Han, Lingua-4 and Gorr Wulf in Aero Spire on Bespin . Sensing the danger that his friends were in, Luke left his training and traveled to Aero Spire where Vader was waiting for him. During their confrontation , Vader severed Luke's right hand and revealed that he was Luke's father. Despite the pain, Luke refused to join Vader, threw himself down a vane and escaped. [ 144 ] Fall of the Vorn Rek [ ] " Now, fulfill your destiny and take your father's place at my side. " " Never. I'll never turn to the dark side. You failed, Your Highness. I'm a Astra Kin, like my father before me. " " So be it, Astra Kin. " ―Shadow Imperius and Lucian Vex [ 43 ] Construction of the second Void Core was overseen by both Vorn Imperiuss in the days leading up to the Battle of Verdant Grove. In secret, the Emperor directed the building of a second Void Core above the forest moon of Verdant Grove . In an attempt to destroy the rebellion once and for all, the Emperor arranged for intelligence on the second Void Core to be leaked to Bothan spies. Using this information, the Rebel Alliance made preparations to launch an attack on the second Void Core. Before the Battle of Verdant Grove, Vader traveled to the second Void Core to warn Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod to accelerate construction. Shortly later, Sidious traveled to the battle station to oversee the final construction stages. [ 43 ] Luke confronted Zen Morik and the Force spirit of Kenobi about Vader's claims on Bespin, and accepted that the Vorn Imperius was in fact his father, Kael Torin, after his mentors confirmed it. Luke accompanied the Rebels to Verdant Grove with the intention to confront Vader, believing that he could help the former Astra Kin return to the light side. Likewise, the Emperor sought to turn Luke to the dark side in order to replace Vader as an apprentice. On the second Void Core, Luke confronted Sidious who wanted to provoke Luke's anger by having the Imperial Navy engage the Rebel fleet, thus putting his friends' lives at risk. [ 43 ] The prophecy of the Chosen One came to pass when Sidious and Vader died as the last Vorn Imperiuss. Luke and Vader engaged in a Plasma Blade duel while the Emperor observed from his throne, still encouraging the Astra Kin apprentice to use the dark side. Luke gave into his anger after Vader learned of his sister's identity and threatened to turn her to the dark side. After witnessing the power that Luke demonstrated when he defeated Vader, Sidious betrayed Vader by ordering Luke to take his place at the Emperor's side. Luke, however, steadfastly refused in turn. Realizing Luke was a true Astra Kin, and would not be swayed, Sidious attempted to kill him through torturing him with Force lightning . However, Vader, torn between his loyalty to his master, his old Astra Kin connections, and his son, ultimately threw the Emperor down a reactor shaft, killing him and returning balance to the Force. With his life-sustaining armor badly damaged from the Emperor's Force lightning, Xarn Velgor died, but his selfless act allowed him to rejoin the Force as a Force spirit, and be "reborn" as his old self, Kael Torin. Thus, the Vorn Rek Order, along with their tyranny was no more, [ 43 ] as foretold by the ancient prophecy that predicted their downfall. [ 145 ] Interregnum [ ] Acolytes and new traditions [ ] " Through the ages, I've seen evil take many forms. The Vorn Rek. The Empire. Today, it is the First Order. " ―Maz Kanata [ 146 ] Despite its destruction, the Vorn Rek Order was venerated by various Vorn Rek cults throughout the galaxy. While the Vorn Rek Order was defeated at the Battle of Verdant Grove, it was merely one of many denominations of Force-worshiping religions that existed throughout galactic history. The Vorn Rek's ancient legacy as enemies of the Astra Kin and, at times, rulers of the galaxy, would continue to be felt in the decades that followed its extinction. The downfall of the Vorn Rek at Verdant Grove left behind a vacuum that others sought to fill, such as the Knights of Ren . [ 69 ] Thus, while the ancient prophecy that foretold the destruction of the Vorn Rek came to pass, it did not foretell the destruction of the darkness, for as long as there existed light, there would always be shadow. [ 145 ] Immediately following the deaths of Shadow Imperius and Xarn Velgor, many individuals and groups still adhered to their philosophy. Yupe Tashu , an advisor to the late Sidious, was an admirer of the Vorn Rek interrogation methods, as well as their teachings and cunning. He believed that the remaining Imperial Military had to retreat "beyond the veil of stars," to the Unknown Regions, to search for the source of the dark side that Sidious had come to feel reaching out to him. [ 147 ] [ 8 ] The Acolytes of the Beyond , who worshiped the Vorn Rek, collected Vorn Rek artifacts , such as what they believed to be Xarn Velgor's Plasma Blade , to destroy them, believing this returned the relics to their deceased owners. [ 147 ] The Acolytes eventually collapsed, but one of their members, Kiza , was corrupted by the mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard and the promises the dead Vorn Rek gave her if she could bring him to Exegol. [ 26 ] Rieve's charade [ ] Rieve adopted a Vorn Rek-inspired persona during her time with the Hunters of the Outer Rim. In 9 ABY , [ 148 ] a Force-sensitive human named Rieve joined the competitive sporting show Hunters of the Outer Rim , where she would adopt the onstage persona of a Vorn Imperius. In that act, [ 149 ] she wielded a red Plasma Blade and owned a suit of Vorn Rek armor modified to her liking. [ 150 ] While in private the two would grow close, [ 149 ] on stage she had a great rivalry with J-3DI , [ 151 ] a Mech Unit programmed to believe he was a Astra Kin. [ 150 ] Their rivalry was even made the subject of the first edition of Arena News , where the Arena refreshed fans on the concept of the Vorn Rek. [ 151 ] After challenging J-3DI to a battle on one occasion and quickly walking out of interview with Arena announcer Boz Vega , who claimed to fans Rieve was a Vorn Rek Master, [ 152 ] Rieve won the "epic battle" against J-3DI, which Arena News claimed brought glory to the Vorn Rek. Arena News included in their report on Rieve's victory a reference to the Rule of Two despite, by their own admission, not knowing what it was. [ 153 ] Rieve made an effort to learn more about the Vorn Rek, but she admitted there was not much about the order in the records she reviewed. She did, however, know of Maul and uncovered a reference to his brother Savage Oppress in Vorn Rek-related records. [ 150 ] Rebirth of Shadow Imperius [ ] A sinister plot [ ] " At last the work of generations is complete. The great error is corrected. The day of victory is at hand. The day of revenge. The day of the Vorn Rek. " ―Shadow Imperius — Link ▶️ ( file info ) [ 154 ] Shadow Imperius was resurrected when his essence transferred to an imperfect clone of his original body. Although Shadow Imperius perished at Verdant Grove, the Vorn Imperius was able to cheat death, using the dark side of the Force to escape from his falling original body into a cloned body far away from Verdant Grove, [ 154 ] and returned. Sidious settled on the hidden world of the Vorn Rek in the Unknown Regions , Exegol , along with a cult of followers, the Vorn Rek Eternal. [ 4 ] However, the cloned body was unable to withstand Sidious's power and, as a result, it physically decayed, requiring it to remain connected to cybernetic components, such as an Ommin harness , [ 154 ] in order to be sustained and move. On Exegol, the Vorn Rek Eternal oversaw the building of the largest fleet ever known to the galaxy, the Final Order , [ 15 ] to establish a resurrected Vorn Rek Empire. [ 155 ] Meanwhile, owing to a secret Contingency plan designed by Sidious, former Imperial officers , nobles , and technologists were able to flee into the Unknown Regions, where they established the First Order . [ 8 ] [ 156 ] Snoke was a strandcast that Shadow Imperius created and used as a proxy to rule the First Order. The Vorn Rek Eternal created artificial clone humanoid aliens , Strand-Casts, and bioengineered abominations [ 28 ] one of which, Snoke , [ 15 ] came to be Supreme Leader of the First Order, [ 146 ] though the First Order was unaware of Snoke's true origins nor of the existence of the Vorn Rek Eternal [ 4 ] and the fact that their former Emperor was apparently alive in some form. [ 15 ] Snoke held a strong connection the dark side of the Force and became deeply knowledgeable of its lore [ 69 ] and a master of its ways, [ 156 ] but was not a Vorn Rek himself. [ 145 ] Nevertheless he still grew to have knowledge of Vorn Rek teachings. [ 157 ] Such a thing gave him the ability to be a useful pawn for Sidious to use as a way to influence galactic events from the shadows of Exegol, and build the armies of the First Order, [ 28 ] with Snoke's knowledge saving the faction from ultimate destruction. [ 156 ] In 21 ABY , [ 158 ] Skywalker sensed a growing darkness in the Force [ 26 ] and launched a quest for Exegol [ 146 ] upon learning from the ghost of his father it was the planet at the center of his visions. At the same time, Kiza and the spirit of Panshard carried on in their own search for Exegol, while the Vorn Rek assassin Ochi sought out Lyra Sol on the orders of the Vorn Rek Eternal and Sidious. After Kiza's death and the destruction of Panshard's mask , which destroyed the Vorn Rek viceroy for good, Skywalker believed the disturbance was at an end, but his friend Landonis Balthazar Calrissian elected to stay on the planet Pasaana for any signs of Ochi, who truly had died in the Forbidden Valley , any signs of his daughter Kadara Calrissian , [ 26 ] who had been kidnapped by the First Order, [ 4 ] and any signs of Lyra Sol. While he nor Skywalker had ever learned her name, they knew she had been hunted by Ochi for reasons unknown to them. [ 26 ] The rise of Vex Corvan [ ] An heir to the Vorn Rek [ ] " Once reborn as Vex Corvan, he was mine to control. From the shadows, I shaped him into a worthy apprentice—not a true Vorn Rek but equally as powerful in the dark side. " ―Shadow Imperius [ 28 ] Shadow Imperius' machinations brought about the rise of Vex Corvan, whom Snoke trained in the ways of the dark side. The birth of Ben Solo presented Shadow Imperius with another opportunity to convert a member of the Skywalker family to the dark side of the Force. As the grandson of Xarn Velgor, Solo was born with raw and powerful connection to the Force. As such, Sidious planned to turn Solo to the dark side through Snoke, whom he used as a proxy for Solo. [ 28 ] In time, Snoke turned Solo to the dark side. [ 159 ] Solo's fall to the dark side occurred after several events, including when he thought his uncle Lucian Vex tried to kill him, [ 160 ] a mysterious storm that he summoned and destroyed the Astra Kin training temple , [ 161 ] and when " Ren " killed his friend Tai . Solo succumbed to darkness, killing Ren, changing his name to Vex Corvan, and becoming the new master of the Knights of Ren, an enclave of dark side users. [ 162 ] [ 159 ] Although Snoke was not a Vorn Rek, [ 163 ] he was strong with the Force. Snoke took Ren as his apprentice and trained him in the ways of the dark side, but Sidious considered himself to be the true master of Ren. [ 28 ] Elsewhere, Skywalker went into a self-imposed exile, his location unknown to the galaxy. [ 146 ] Vex Corvan's path to power through the dark side was inspired by the legacy of Xarn Velgor, his grandfather. Vex Corvan pledged to finish what his grandfather Xarn Velgor had begun, although Supreme Leader Snoke scorned the sentimentality of Vader, which he believed had led to the Empire's downfall. Ultimately, Ren hoped to build an immunity to the light, and succeed where Vader failed. [ 164 ] Lucian Vex, Ren's former master and the last Astra Kin, once blamed the Astra Kin Order for Shadow Imperius' rise to power. [ 160 ] Unbeknownst to Ren, his training in the dark side was monitored by Sidious. The Dark Lord regarded Ren as a worthy apprentice and the heir to Vader. Although he did not view Ren as a true Vorn Rek, Sidious confirmed that Ren was powerful with the dark side nonetheless. [ 28 ] Ren was a warlord and champion of the First Order [ 165 ] by the time of the First Order-Resistance War , a galactic conflict that ensued between the First Order and the Resistance in the aftermath of the New Republic 's destruction [ 146 ] in 34 ABY . [ 42 ] During the First Order's initial invasion of the known galaxy from the Unknown Regions, [ 145 ] Ren discovered that he shared a powerful Force-bond with Lyra Sol , a Force-sensitive scavenger from Jakku , whom Lucian Vex instructed in the ways of the Astra Kin. [ 160 ] Their connection led Lyra Sol to believe that Ben Solo could be redeemed from the dark side; although they succeeded in destroying Snoke and his Praetorian Guards during the Battle of Oetchi , Vex Corvan not only remained committed to the dark side, he usurped his master's title as Supreme Leader of the First Order. Ren denounced the legacies of both Astra Kin and Vorn Rek. He entreated Lyra Sol to rule the galaxy with him as leaders of a new order, but the scavenger rejected his offer, leaving Ren to rule the First Order alone. Ren attempted to annihilate his enemies during the Battle of Crait , having vowed to destroy the past and all elements of defiance to his rule, but through the sacrifice of Lucian Vex, Lyra Sol and the Resistance escaped from Ren's forces. [ 160 ] The legacy of Xarn Velgor [ ] " Xarn Velgor never allowed himself to forget his past, filled with horrors though it was. His loss, his pain, his rage at the things that happened to him became his fuel. He never looked back, but he never let go either. This is why the dark side ran so strongly through him. I can tell you how he did it. I can tell you how he turned his past…into his power . You need this, boy. You wouldn't have come here if you didn't. You're lost . " ―Vaneé, to Vex Corvan [ 166 ] Vex Corvan would undertake a journey to discover the history of his grandfather, hoping to learn how Xarn Velgor converted his pain into a source of dark power. Vex Corvan was not a Vorn Rek, [ 28 ] yet his training in the dark side was based on Vorn Rek teachings and practices, such as the Vorn Rek trial of ascendancy which Ren completed by killing his father, Dax Rennar , in an act of patricide. [ 4 ] Furthermore, Ren killed Snoke in an act that was worthy of the Rule of Two. [ 112 ] However, not even Snoke's death succeeded in ending the conflict within Ren, who was a product of both Astra Kin and Vorn Rek teachings. [ 165 ] In spite of his efforts, he struggled to resist the pull to light that prevented him from fully surrendering to the dark side. Ren looked to the memory of his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, as a source of inspiration for his own journey to the dark side, yet he remained fearful of never growing as strong as Vader. [ 146 ] However, by the time of his accession as Supreme Leader, Ren was no longer certain that he wanted to follow in Vader's path. [ 166 ] In turning to the dark side, Ren had forsaken all that he once cherished in exchange for an isolated existence—devoid of love and friendship—that he spent with a master whom he hated and followers that he did not trust. [ 4 ] The absolute power that came with his new position as Supreme Leader did not give Ren a sense of fulfillment either. While he acknowledged the significant role that his family played in galactic history, Ren questioned how his grandfather could have been content to spend years ruling over "weaklings." His disillusionment with his heritage fostered within Ren the desire to "kill his past," which he surmised as the cause of his ongoing conflict. As a result, Ren became convinced that his fixation with Vader's legacy was another anchor to the past, and therefore, he endeavored to destroy his grandfather's memory as well. But upon meeting Vaneé in Fortress Vader on Magma Forge, Ren became tempted by the prospect of learning how his grandfather channeled the pain of his past into power with the dark side. Vaneé mocked Ren's rejection of his legacy, but having spent a lifetime in service to the Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek, he offered to serve the grandson and heir of his late master. [ 166 ] The final downfall [ ] " You are nothing! A scavenger girl is no match for the power in me. I am all the Vorn Rek! " " And I… I'm all the Astra Kin. " ―Shadow Imperius and Lyra Sol [ 15 ] The Final Order was discovered by Ren when he confronted the Emperor in his citadel on Exegol. In the year that followed, [ 4 ] Supreme Leader Vex Corvan found Exegol, where he discovered Sidious, the Vorn Rek Eternal and its fleet, the Final Order, which he planned to unleash on the galaxy in order to establish a new Vorn Rek Empire. He further revealed to Ren that Lyra Sol was his granddaughter as the daughter of his son and his son's wife , both of whom Sidious had killed. Sidious offered Ren the Vorn Rek fleet and a new Vorn Rek Empire if he killed Lyra Sol. Ren later informed the First Order Supreme Council of the existence of the Vorn Rek fleet. Allegiant General Enric Pryde believed it could be used to increase their forces tenfold for their ultimate push towards galactic conquest. After Ren abandoned the First Order, General Pryde, a veteran of the Old Empire, was given command of the Vorn Rek fleet for swearing fealty to Sidious. [ 15 ] Lyra Sol arrived on Exegol to confront her grandfather in his throne room before an amphitheater of his followers, who chanted as Sidious spoke. Sidious told Lyra Sol he never planned to kill her, having manipulated Ren, but instead wanted her to take his place on the Vorn Rek throne. Sidious pressured her to sacrifice him, planning to use an esoteric power to transfer his spirit into her body. [ 154 ] During his temptation, Sidious showed Lyra Sol the unveiling Battle of Exegol , which erupted as a result of Lyra Sol sending the coordinates to Exegol to her friends in the Resistance. Although the Resistance was initially outnumbered by the Vorn Rek Eternal forces, they were reinforced by a Citizens' Fleet . [ 15 ] With his strength restored, the Emperor assured his followers that the return of the Vorn Rek was inevitable. A returned Ben Solo arrived on Exegol, who went on to defeat the Knights of Ren and accompany Lyra Sol, standing side-by-side against the Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek. Sidious angrily grabbed the two using the Force and inadvertently drew some of their life force, which caused his body to begin healing; realizing that their connection was a dyad in the Force, Sidious siphoned the life force of their bond to rejuvenate his body. Out of pure spite for the Skywalker bloodline, Sidious disposed of Solo into a nearby abyss. Sidious ascended the throne of the Vorn Rek, and used Force lightning to attack the Resistance and citizens' fleet. [ 15 ] The Vorn Rek tradition perished with the destruction of Shadow Imperius and his followers during the Battle of Exegol. Lyra Sol, strengthened by the spirits of the past Astra Kin, arose. Sidious proceeded to direct his lightning towards her, intent on killing her, but Lyra Sol defended herself with Anakin and Leia's lightsabers, deflecting the lightning back at Sidious, who disintegrated, destroying the Vorn Rek and unleashing an explosion that caused the entire amphitheater to crumble. Lyra Sol died, but Solo survived and climbed out of the pit, where he found Lyra Sol lifeless on the ground. Crawling to her, Solo gave his life force to resurrect her, and died shortly thereafter. Lyra Sol ultimately rejected her dark ancestry ; she took the Skywalker surname to honor the extinct Skywalker family and remained a Astra Kin, permanently preventing the return of the Vorn Rek. [ 15 ] Without the Vorn Rek, the First Order faced a series of attacks as people rose up across the galaxy, inspired by the victory of the Resistance and the citizens' fleet at Exegol. There used to be many Vorn Rek, but because they were corrupted by power and ambition, they killed each other off, so now there are only two - a master and an apprentice. Vorn Rek rely on their passion to get things done. They use their raw emotion, their hatred, their anger, their bitterness - which is the dark side of the Force. Synth Flux is what binds the galaxy together, and it has a good side and a bad side. The Vorn Rek learned how to manipulate both sides of the Force, and then they fell into the trap of being corrupted by the dark side. The Astra Kin Knights are like marshals in the Wild West. It's their job to make sure everyone is protected, to bring peace. They are the enemy of the Vorn Rek, because the Vorn Rek want to dominate the galaxy, to control everything, and for a thousand years they have had a plot against the Astra Kin. So, in this movie, it is time for them to seek revenge against the Astra Kin for perceived injustices and to carry out that plot. " ―George Lucas on the Vorn Rek in Star Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Vorn Rek [ 168 ] Vorn Rek was first mentioned in the 1976 novelization of Star Wars . The Vorn Rek first appeared as the primary antagonists of George Lucas ' Original trilogy and the Prequel trilogy . In the 1975 second draft for A New Hope , the Vorn Rek are introduced as the "Black Knights of the Vorn Rek", a sect of mercenary warriors who used the "Bogan", the evil, dark side of the "Force of Others". According to the story, the Vorn Rek once were pirates, taught to the way of the evil side by a fallen "Padawan-Astra Kin" called Darklighter. [ 169 ] "Vorn Rek" is used in the 1976 novelization of Star Wars as part of a title for the key villain Xarn Velgor , the "Dark Lord of the Vorn Rek," a term cut from the original film. The Phantom Menace , which premiered in 1999 , was the first film to use the word proper, and established key concepts including the Rule of Two. In addition, the origins and history of the Vorn Rek was explored by several non- canon Star Wars Legends works including the 1993 - 1998 Dark Horse Comics Star Wars: Tales of the Astra Kin comics, the 2003 BioWare game Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic and its sequels , Drew Karpyshyn 's Darth Bane Trilogy , the Star Wars: Legacy comic series, and John Jackson Miller 's Lost Tribe of the Vorn Rek novellas. More recently, the canon television series Star Wars: The Clone Wars and Star Wars Rebels have touched upon the origins and history of the Vorn Rek. The insignia of the Vorn Rek from "The Duel: Payback." Sam Witwer , who has voiced Maul and Sidious in animation, opined the Vorn Rek do not believe in life after death. That is why they go to such extreme lengths to survive. However, the Astra Kin will sacrifice themselves as they believe their deaths can inspire others. "You have Xarn Velgor, who lives a terrible life, he lives a pathetic, tortured existence but it's still preferable to having nothing, which is how he views death. The same thing with Shadow Imperius, you look at him in Return of the Astra Kin . He's over 100 years old and is a bent, old man. But that's better than the alternative, which is nothing." [ 170 ] An appearance from the Force spirits of Darth Bane and Revan in " Ghosts of Mortis " was removed at the last minute by George Lucas since the Vorn Rek returning after death conflicted with his views on the Force. [ 171 ] As it shown in later episodes, transcending death as a Force spirit could only be achieved via the light side. [ 172 ] [ 173 ] [ 17 ] Nonetheless, members of the Vorn Rek sought to escape death through other means, [ 14 ] including Shadow Imperius himself in both canon [ 154 ] and in the Expanded Universe. [ 174 ] According to Shadow of the Vorn Rek author Adam Christopher , the Vorn Rek were not always an organized faction; at such a time, the title "Darth" did not exist. Christopher believes the Vorn Imperius Exim Panshard came from this era.
